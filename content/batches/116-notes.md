# Batch 116 — A2 speaking, twelve new tasks (22 November 2026)

Three tasks of each official DUO "Spreken A2" type (blueprint §4.3): three `video-answer`, three `picture-describe`, three `picture-choose`, three `picture-sequence`. The bank grows from 83 to 95. All text was written for this project; official material was used for structure only. Nothing here is a KNM claim or a time-sensitive fact. Levels are authoring targets (`status: "draft"`, `targetLevelValidated: false`); this is AI editorial work and claims no CEFR validation or exam equivalence. Record shape copies the current batch093 records, without media or derived fields.

Shared: `speakingSeconds: 40`, `prepSeconds: 10`, `rubric: a2-spreken`, three criteria per task mirroring the type. Models are 34–42 words (target 25–45), main clauses with at most one `want`/`dat`/`als` clause, no sentence over 14 words. Each sample misses exactly one criterion (one `null` quote, the others verbatim) and carries one or two typical learner errors.

**Tone.** All twelve are ordinary everyday tasks; none is humorous (the plan marks no HUMOUR item for this batch).

**Organisations and names.** No firm, shop, school or web address is named (bibliotheek, sportschool, apotheek, fabriek, markt, supermarkt, zwembad are generic), so there was nothing to check on the web. Names come from the pool: Anouk (items 1 and 6), Marco (2), Tijmen (5), Leyla (4), Guus (11), Derya (10), Hester (8), Carmen (9, brief only), Reza (12, brief only). Each name appears in at most two items. No voice name and no older cast member is used.

## 3. Per task

### Video-answer (speaker named first in the situation, one spoken question, one still)

1. **bibliotheek-boek.** Prompt: "Anouk werkt in de bibliotheek. U staat bij de balie. Zij stelt u een vraag. U hoort de vraag. Geef antwoord. Zeg ook hoe lang u het boek wilt lenen." Cue (`f-mature`, Ruth): "Goedemiddag! Wat voor boek zoekt u? Is het voor u of voor een kind?" Change from the plan: "voor uzelf" is outside the A2 word gate, and the cue is gated, so it became "voor u". Third criterion (one more detail) is the loan period, asked in the prompt. Sample (22 words) omits the loan period; errors "van de koken" (het koken) and a comma splice. Model 34 words.
2. **sportschool-intake.** Cue (`m-young`, Ruben): "Welkom bij de sportschool! Hoe vaak wilt u sporten? En waarom wilt u gaan sporten?" The extra detail is the day, asked in the prompt ("Zeg ook op welke dag u wilt komen"). The reason is a personal choice, no real data. Sample omits the why; errors "ik wil sport" (verb missing) and "want dan ik werk niet" (word order). Model 37 words.
3. **apotheek-ophalen.** Distinct from `batch001-apotheek` (picture-describe: Roos, a picture of the counter). Here the assistant says the new medicine is ready tomorrow and asks time and who collects it (`f-adult`, Lieke). The extra detail is a question the learner asks, how often to take it, which fits the pharmacy and also gives the learner a real thing to ask. The prompt says so ("Vraag ook hoe vaak u het medicijn moet nemen"). The assistant is unnamed in prompt and cue because "Carmen" is not on the name list (the gate failed it); see Requested words. Sample omits the question; errors "op twee uur" (om) and "mijn man hij werkt" (doubled subject). Model 37 words.

### Picture-describe

4. **ziekenhuis-bezoek.** Prompt: "Leyla bezoekt haar vader in het ziekenhuis. Vertel wat u ziet. Noem twee dingen die mensen vaak meenemen als ze op bezoek gaan. Vertel ook wat u zelf meeneemt bij zo'n bezoek. Gebruik het plaatje." The picture shows flowers and a bowl of green grapes, but the prompt names neither. Change from the plan: "a bag of grapes" became "a bowl of green grapes", because the local model draws bags badly and a bowl on the bedside table stays readable at display size. The room is calm and the father smiles; no illness is shown (no drip, no monitor). Words: "druiven" and "fruit" passed the gate. Sample omits the two things; error "in de bed". Model 40 words names bloemen and fruit/druiven, a card, and what the speaker brings.
5. **sneeuw-straat.** Change from the plan: criterion 3 "what you think of snow" would have named the snow, a part of what the first criterion asks the learner to say. Clothing is visible in the picture: the man wears a woolly hat and red jacket, the children thick coats, hats and scarves. ## 4. Language control

- Word gate: all learner-visible fields pass `config/a2-vocabulary.json` + A1; the first run failed on mezelf, spannend, uzelf, carmen, schep, slee, trottoir, branden, schijnt, schappen, buigt, medewerkster, klappen, rand, reza; all replaced (see items). Starters use listed words only.
- Reported compounds, judged: `ziekenhuiskamer` (ziekenhuis + kamer): transparent. `zwemlerares` (zwemmen + lerares): transparent. Both appear only in alts.
- Warning for the `kinderboerderij` slug: see item 6; different part and different task type.
- Prompts: video-answer prompts follow "… stelt u een vraag. U hoort de vraag. Geef antwoord." and ask only the third criterion; the cue asks the other two. Picture-describe prompts say "Vertel wat u ziet." before "Noem twee …". Picture-choose prompts end with "Kies een van de plaatjes. Vertel ook waarom. Zeg ook iets over het andere plaatje." Sequence prompts end "Kijk naar de plaatjes. Vertel wat X doet. Vertel iets over alle plaatjes." No title, situation or starter states what a picture should supply.

2. Item 7 pair: day against night must be unmistakable at display size (sunlit windows against black windows with lamps).
3. Item 10 panel 1: the phone must be visibly lying on the stones with the crack drawn as a dark zigzag; the local model draws broken things poorly, so expect a second try or the API fallback.
4. Item 12 panel 2: three other children must be countable; panel 3 shows four people and a cake, the most crowded picture of the batch.
5. Item 11 panel 3: the paper bag must read as a small white bag, not a box.
6. Two Anouks and two Carmens as noted in §2.
