## 2. Sources of the facts

None of the texts cites a source; all organisations, people and figures are invented. Real Dutch practice was mirrored where a learner might meet it outside the exam, so that the practice text teaches nothing false:

- **Text 1 (klachtenafhandeling).** The legal paragraph follows Dutch consumer sales law in plain words: a product must be "deugdelijk" (conformity, art. 7:17 BW: it must do what the buyer may reasonably expect, which depends on the product and the price); a product that fails too soon gives a right to free repair or replacement and, if that fails within a reasonable time, to money back (art. 7:21–7:22 BW); the seller is the contracting party and may not send the customer to the manufacturer; any proof of purchase counts, a receipt is not required; a manufacturer's guarantee is extra and does not end the legal rights; there is no legal right to return a sound product bought in a shop (exchange is a shop service), while distance purchases carry a fourteen-day cooling-off period (art. 6:230o BW). The burden-of-proof rule (defects in the first twelve months are presumed present at delivery) was left out deliberately; the example says only that a 300-euro coffee machine may be expected to last years, which is the conformity test. The three-step process, the five-working-day answer, the split between what an employee may settle and what the owner decides, and the follow-up call are common practice presented as a textbook recommendation ("Een goede afspraak is …"), not as law. "Ervaren ondernemers weten dat de meeste ontevreden klanten niets zeggen" states a widely taught marketing point without an invented statistic.
- **Text 2 (gratis-zwemles).** The background is real: school swimming has disappeared in most municipalities and lessons are paid by parents; a diploma A course costs several hundred euros and the full A-B-C series over a thousand; support for low-income families exists (jeugdfonds-type schemes) with an income limit; the "just above the limit" gap is a familiar argument. The figures (600 euro, 1,000 euro, "bijna verdubbeld", "twee jaar geleden", the three-absences rule) are invented, and "gemeente Westerhout" is the bank's fictional town (used as a region and town in batches 004 and 011; no municipality of that name exists — web-checked, see section 8). The writer's position is a policy opinion the coordinator asked for; it names no party, politician or real programme. Water safety is mentioned soberly ("ongelukken in het water", "de brandweer die te water gaat"); no accident is described.
- **Text 3 (examenreglement).** The structure mirrors the examination regulations language schools publish for their own course exams: portal registration with a deadline and a late fee, original identity document, a grace period for written parts and none for recorded parts, a cancellation rule for illness, results in a portal, inspection by appointment, a limited number of re-sits with a validity period, a fee list. Every figure is invented (ten days, 20 euro, 15 euro, fifteen minutes, ten working days, grade 6, four weeks, two weeks, thirty minutes, two re-sits, twelve months, 35 euro, 12,50 euro, 9.00–16.00, 088 456 78 90). "Mijn Berkenpoort" is a portal name, not a URL. The regulation is the school's own; it does not describe the Staatsexamen NT2 or DUO, so nothing in it can be mistaken for an official rule.

## 5. Questions: keys, operations and distractor rationales

Skill spread over 20 questions: detail 7, rule-application 5, purpose 3, inference 2, summary 1, quantity 1, time-place 1. Texts 1 and 2 each carry an inference item and at least two detail items whose evidence is a passage of two to four sentences; text 3 carries six persona scanning questions (a quantity item that combines two figures, four rule applications, a time-place item) and then the purpose question, as the look-up paragraph of §4.6 asks. Purpose keys: informeren (descriptief), waarschuwen (persuasief, see section 8), uitleggen (instructief).

### Item 1 — klachtenafhandeling (keys A B C A C B C)

- **q1 detail, key A** "Het bedrijf kan de fout dan nog goedmaken en de klant houden." Operation: the reason in the introduction, two sentences ("de meeste ontevreden klanten niets zeggen … Een klant die wél klaagt, geeft het bedrijf de kans …"); "goedmaken" paraphrases "herstellen". B "Het bedrijf ziet dan welke producten de klanten het liefst kopen" — anchored in the step-3 sentence about patterns, which reveal a bad product or supplier, not favourites. C "De medewerkers leren dan om rustig te blijven bij een boze klant" — anchored in step 1 ("ook als die boos is"); staying calm is an instruction, not the reason a complaint is valuable.
- **q2 detail, key B** "Zij laat de klant uitpraten en schrijft daarna de feiten op." Operation: the sequence in the first three sentences of step 1. A "Zij belooft meteen een oplossing, zodat de klant rustig wordt" — reversed: "belooft nog niets". C "Zij stuurt de klant door naar de eigenaar, die de klacht beoordeelt" — anchored in step 2, which opens with "Niet elke klacht hoeft naar de baas".
- **q3 detail, key C** "De medewerker regelt kleine zaken; over grote beslist de eigenaar." Operation: the roles paragraph, three sentences; "kleine zaken" and "grote" paraphrase the examples (omruilen, korting / dure reparatie, geld terug). A "De eigenaar beoordeelt elke klacht, en de medewerker geeft het antwoord door" — overgeneralisation of "Die bekijkt het product … en beoordeelt"; contradicts "Niet elke klacht hoeft naar de baas". B "De medewerker beslist zelf, tenzij de klant erom vraagt de eigenaar te spreken" — the customer's wish plays no role in the text; the split is by the size of the case.
- **q4 rule-application, key A** "De winkel hoeft de jas niet terug te nemen; ruilen is een service." Operation: apply "ruilen is geen recht" to a sound product bought in a shop; the case states the shop purchase, the sound product ("de kleur toch niet mooi") and the next-day return so that only this rule bites. B "De winkel moet de jas terugnemen, omdat Roos binnen veertien dagen terugkomt" — applies the online cooling-off period to a shop purchase. C "De winkel moet de jas terugnemen als Roos de kassabon kan laten zien" — the receipt sentence is about proof of purchase for a defective product, not a right to return a sound one.
- **q5 detail, key C** "Als reparatie of een nieuw exemplaar niet op tijd lukt." Operation: the two-step rule in the law paragraph ("recht op gratis reparatie of een nieuw exemplaar. Lukt dat niet binnen een redelijke tijd, dan …"); "op tijd" paraphrases "binnen een redelijke tijd". A "Als het product binnen een jaar na de aankoop kapotgaat" — the common guarantee-year misconception; a product that fails too soon gives a right to repair or replacement first, not to money back. B "Als de klant de kassabon van de aankoop nog kan laten zien" — a receipt only proves the purchase.
- **q6 inference, key B** "Dat de rechten van een klant niet stoppen als de fabrieksgarantie afloopt." Operation: function of the example; the example illustrates the "Ten tweede" rule and says "Dat is onjuist" about the seller's guarantee-year answer. A "Dat een klant een kapot apparaat het beste binnen een jaar kan terugbrengen" — draws the wrong lesson from "veertien maanden" and "een jaar". C "Dat een verkoper een dure reparatie beter aan de fabrikant kan overlaten" — contradicts "mag de klant hiervoor niet doorsturen naar de fabrikant".
- **q7 purpose, key C** "De lezer informeren over het afhandelen van klachten en de eisen van de wet." Descriptief; the introduction announces exactly this. A "overtuigen dat een bedrijf elke klant altijd gelijk moet geven" — the text allows an unfounded complaint ("Is de klacht niet terecht, dan …"). B "waarschuwen voor klanten die de garantie … willen misbruiken" — no misuse is mentioned; the warning, if any, is aimed at sellers who refuse rightly.

## 8. Things the reviewer should look at

- **Purpose key of the opinion piece (item 2 q6) is waarschuwen, not overtuigen.** The coordinator's brief allowed waarschuwen or overtuigen and asked to vary from the existing batches (which keyed overtuigen once and informeren/uitleggen five times). The text was therefore built as a warning with a remedy: it calls itself a warning in the first paragraph, the danger frames the opening and the closing, and the free-lessons proposal is presented as the way to avert it. The overtuigen distractor states the opponents' position, so it is false in content, not only in verb. If the reviewer judges that a persuasive text must key overtuigen, the swap is local: key "De lezer overtuigen dat elke gemeente gratis zwemles voor kinderen moet betalen." with distractors "De lezer waarschuwen voor het zwemmen in open water zonder diploma B." and "De lezer informeren over de kosten van een zwemdiploma in verschillende gemeenten."; evidence "Daarom vind ik dat elke gemeente gratis zwemles moet aanbieden aan alle kinderen." (then q5's evidence should move to the closing sentence to avoid reuse).
- **Item 1, legal simplification.** The law paragraph omits the burden-of-proof rule (a defect in the first twelve months is presumed to have existed at delivery) and states the remedies in the order the law gives them. A reviewer with legal knowledge may want "binnen een redelijke tijd" kept exactly as it is (it is the evidence of q5) and may check that "Dat is onjuist" in the example is acceptable without the proof nuance; the example's product (300 euro, fourteen months) was chosen so that the expectation test is not in doubt.
- **Item 1 q5, distractor A** "Als het product binnen een jaar na de aankoop kapotgaat" is wrong because it names the wrong remedy (repair or replacement comes first), not because the year is wrong.
