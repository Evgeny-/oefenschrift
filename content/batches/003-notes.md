# Batch 003: design and review notes

Batch 003 contains 32 original draft exercises: eight KNM, eight reading, eight writing and eight speaking. Reading, writing and speaking each contain four A2 and four B1 items. KNM uses `level: "A2"` internally and covers stable civic guidance from current official Dutch government pages.

## Design aims

Every closed question has an `evidence` value copied exactly from its item text. This makes key review mechanical and keeps explanations traceable. KNM items also carry an official source note, URL and review date. The exercise wording and scenarios are original.

A2 items use short, explicit messages and direct practical actions. B1 reading items connect conditions, sequence, cause and consequence across paragraphs. B1 open tasks require explaining a situation and proposing or negotiating a response.

Each open item has three bilingual criteria. Its sample demonstrates two goals and omits one. The two non-null `quotes` are exact, case-sensitive sample substrings; the complete model covers all three goals.

## Repeatable authoring and review process

1. Define the batch matrix, topic spread, target level and schema before drafting.
2. For KNM, choose stable facts from current official sources. Record the URL, review date and a concise source note. Avoid pending policy and changing monetary thresholds.
3. Draft original situations without copying official exam wording. Put every fact needed for a fixed key in the item text.
4. Write the correct answer first, then distractors tied to nearby details. Reject any distractor defensible through ordinary assumptions.
5. Copy an exact supporting substring into each closed question's `evidence`.
6. For open tasks, define three observable goals. Write a sample covering exactly two and map exact substrings plus one `null`. Write a model covering all three.
7. Run automated checks for counts, IDs, option keys, answer keys, evidence substrings, quote substrings, omitted goals, status and target-level flags.
8. Conduct a separate editorial review of Dutch naturalness, factual accuracy, answer uniqueness, distractors and A2/B1 distinction. Revise and rerun all checks.
9. Keep accepted items in draft until broader language review, blueprint mapping and learner validation are complete.

## Limitations

The level labels are unvalidated authoring targets. These questions do not claim official equivalence and cannot support a pass score or readiness prediction. Official sources support civic facts only; they do not validate the exercise design. Source facts need periodic rechecking, even when chosen for stability. Writing and speaking models are examples, and other understandable responses may satisfy the criteria.
