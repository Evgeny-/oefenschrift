# Batch 039 author notes

Migration of all 13 legacy A2 reading exercises. Each entire text and question set was freshly authored; exact exercise IDs and scenario titles are retained as explicitly requested. Legacy fields are removed. No audio or images are required for these selected task types. This is original practice with an unvalidated A2 authoring target.

## Provenance and names

Blueprint and the local official-exam analysis were used for structure only. All service arrangements, dates, prices and people are fictional. No legal, medical treatment or official institutional rules are taught. The veterinary item replaces dosing instructions with reading a label and asking the practice for clarification.

The only invented named organisation is Fietsatelier Velmora. Exact web search `"Fietsatelier Velmora"` on 2026-09-11 returned no results. This checks obvious name collisions, not legal name clearance. Legacy organisation names and addresses were removed; other institutions are generic unnamed shops, a practice, a sports hall or similar. No real addresses, websites or telephone numbers are included.

- q1 (time-place), key A: Woensdag is de winkel gesloten. Donderdag kan zij vanaf 9.00 uur terecht. Evidence: “Op woensdag is onze winkel dicht. Vanaf donderdag zijn we weer open, van 9.00 tot 18.00 uur.”
  - B: Uses reopening time on closed day.
  - C: Uses pickup day after closing.
  - D: Uses closing time as opening time.
- q2 (rule-application), key B: Zonder bon laat zij haar identiteitsbewijs zien. De bankpas is voor het betalen. Evidence: “Bent u de bon kwijt? Laat dan uw identiteitsbewijs zien.”
  - A: Payment object is confused with identification.
  - C: Optional returned object is confused with identification.
- q3 (purpose), key D: De reparatie is klaar. Het bericht vertelt wanneer en hoe zij de fiets kan ophalen. Evidence: “Uw fiets is klaar.”
  - A: The mechanic has already checked them.
  - B: The old band may be taken home, not brought.
  - C: Staff bring the bicycle outside the shop, not to her home.
