# Batch 001: design notes

This batch contains eight original A2 pilot exercises: two each for reading, listening, writing and speaking. The situations are common adult-life contexts and were written for this project rather than adapted from official exam questions.

## Design aims

- Keep instructions, source texts and expected responses short enough for an A2 learner.
- Test practical comprehension through times, places, reasons and required actions.
- Give each closed question one answer supported directly by the source and two plausible distractors.
- Keep the answer-bearing detail in listening transcripts neutral so later audio does not reveal the key through emphasis.
- Give writing and speaking tasks three concrete communication goals. Each sample demonstrates two goals and omits one, while the model covers all three.
- Use exact substrings from each sample in `quotes`, with `null` for the missing goal.
- Mark every item as `draft` until language, task-design and any future audio review are complete.

## Limitations

These are original pilot questions. They have not been validated against learner performance or mapped to a fully audited official exam blueprint. They do not claim equivalence with official examinations and cannot support a pass prediction or validated pass score.

The listening items currently contain transcripts only. Audio must be generated from the approved text, checked in full by a Dutch-language reviewer, and added as a versioned asset before publication. Dates, times, numbers, negation, pacing and answer-revealing emphasis require particular attention.

The writing and speaking examples show one possible response. Other clear A2 responses may satisfy the criteria. Automated feedback, if added later, should assess each criterion independently and must not require exact wording from the model answer.
