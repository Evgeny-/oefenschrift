## 2. Sources and facts mirrored, and premise details

No facts come from official practice exams. The practical rules are ordinary Dutch practice, stated as fiction in an unnamed town:

1. **vakantie-aanvragen.** Employers commonly ask for summer leave early and refuse a week when too many colleagues are already away; leave is entered in an app or HR system and the manager puts it in the rooster. The conversation happens in November 2026 about July 2027; the dates are real calendar days (Monday 5, 12 and 19 July 2027; Sunday 18 and 25 July 2027, checked with a date library). Two weeks off use ten of Rachid's fifteen remaining days.
2. **huiswerk-juf.** Teachers routinely advise parents of a slow reader in groep 4 to read together every day for a short time and to take turns reading. Children's library membership is free in most Dutch municipalities; the reading hour with a volunteer is invented. Primary schools commonly finish at twelve on Wednesday.
3. **woning-bekijken.** Private landlords ask for a recent payslip at a viewing to check income; gas and electricity are often paid separately; the contract follows later. Rent €850 and energy about €100 are realistic for a flat in 2026–2027.
4. **fysio-verzet.** Physiotherapy practices move appointments when a therapist is on a course, ask patients to bring a towel and loose clothing, and often receive the GP's referral letter directly. Phone hours on weekday mornings are common.
5. **boek-gereserveerd.** Dutch libraries hold a reserved item for about a week, lend for three weeks and can send an item to another branch on request. Opening hours with one late evening are typical.
6. **tram-omleiding.** Tram operators announce shortened routes during track work, with a connecting bus and a longer journey time; a valid tram ticket being accepted on the bus is a common arrangement in such diversions.
7. **toernooi-sporthal.** Youth sports days announce blocking cars, lunch in the canteen and the prize ceremony over the hall speakers.
8. **printer-uitleg.** Office printers with "follow-me" printing release documents when a staff pass is held at the reader; colour printing often needs a manager's permission.
9. **intake-taalschool.** Language schools start with an intake (a short computer test and a talk), offer day and evening groups and allow payment in instalments; many employers pay for Dutch lessons. The price (€540 for twelve weeks, or three times €180) is invented and plausible for a smaller school. The item makes no claim about DUO loans or municipal payment, which depend on the learner's legal situation (blueprint §9: no individual legal advice).
10. **bibliotheek-verhuist.** Libraries close for about two weeks during a move and waive fines for items due during the closure. The dates are real Saturdays: 9 and 23 January 2027.
11. **fietsenwinkel-reclame.** Bicycle shops advertise a spring service at a fixed price, same-day repair when the bike comes in early, coffee while you wait and parts charged separately.
12. **rijschool-reclame.** Driving schools advertise to adults and nervous learners, often with a cheaper first lesson; €55 per hour is in line with 2026 prices.

Premise details adjusted from the plan, with reasons:

- **tram-omleiding:** the plan says "bus twelve". The catalogue item `A2:listening:batch005-bus:1` already is a diversion announcement for "lijn 12" with a "Stadhuisplein" stop. To keep the two apart, the connecting bus is **bus achttien** and the last stop is **halte Wilgenplein**. The ticket sentence was also reworded because its first wording matched six-word phrases in concurrent batch 090 (`bus-voor-trein`) and reading `batch020-spoorwerk`.
- **toernooi-sporthal:** "toernooi" is not on the A2 list, so the speaker says "onze basketbaldag voor kinderen". The slug stays as assigned.
- **boek-gereserveerd:** "you can also borrow it at the other branch" became "call us, and we bring the book to Noord within two days". That is how libraries actually handle a change of pick-up branch.
- **printer-uitleg:** "colour only with permission" became "Printen in kleur mag alleen als je chef het goed vindt."
- **intake-taalschool:** "who can pay it" became the employer, or the learner in three instalments.
