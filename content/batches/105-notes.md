## 2. Premise changes and why

- **1 energie-bellen:** the plan's "new amount applies from the next month" clashed with "from 1 March". Now: 95 euro up to and including February, 118 from 1 March (23 more, said aloud so the distractor exists); after the meter numbers a lower amount follows "vanaf april". Reason: two different start dates must be separable.
- **2 internet-kiezen:** "installatie" is unlisted; the employee says the technician "sluit uw internet gratis aan" (40 euro without contract). The technician offers Thursday 8-12 or Friday 14-16 so that the chosen day has a real competitor.
- **3 stadspas:** added "zwemmen kost normaal zes euro" and "de pas zelf is gratis" so the answers have said alternatives. Respectful: "voor mensen met weinig geld", no judgement. Amara asks about papers for her children; the employee says that is not needed (the distractor).
- **4 dienst-eerder:** the speaker says "je" (colleague). Added "begint niet om zeven uur maar om zes uur" and a lunch line for a third said alternative. Phone number is the exam-style placeholder 06 12 34 56 78, spelled digit by digit.
- **5 ziekenhuis-lift:** "poli" and "begane grond" avoided: "de oogarts" and "beneden". A second dead entrance (hoofdingang and achteringang) gives three said entrance options; the working lifts are "aan de zijkant, naast het café" ("noordkant/oostkant" are unlisted). Blood tests stay downstairs until four o'clock; the café closes at five instead of seven.
- **6 ventileren:** "ventileren", "schimmel", "vochtig" avoided: "ramen wijd open", "zwarte plekken" ("vlekken" is unlisted). Added "een half uur is niet nodig, want dan wordt het huis te koud" so the half-hour distractor is said.
- **7 school-app:** added "op vrijdag moeten de kinderen hun werk inleveren" as the competing day for the homework question.
- **8 ov-uitchecken:** the company says "bij onze bussen" and "bij ons" and gives its own fare (2.50 euro) and 5 euro fee, so it cannot be taken for the national rule. "Kaartlezer" was replaced by "het apparaat bij de deur". Added half price for 4 to 11 and full price from 12 so that the free-travel question has said alternatives.
- **9 zorg-werkdag:** the paid course is phrased "volgt eerst een cursus en krijgt daarvoor gewoon salaris", so "betaalde cursus" cannot be heard as "free course". Added lunch at twelve for the time question's distractors.
- **10 buurtsuper-sluit:** Kruisstraat and Dorpsplein kept; the town and the chain are unnamed, so no real shop is meant. The shop on the last day closes at six; Dorpsplein now until nine instead of eight. The delivery is free, not the groceries ("brengt de winkel de boodschappen gratis naar huis").
- **11 verhuisbus:** dates and prices as planned: 49 euro Monday to Thursday, 59 euro Friday to Sunday. "Inbegrepen" avoided ("zitten in de prijs"). Humour: only the closing lines about friends.
- **12 binnenspeeltuin:** the plan's "the weekend" was not needed; added "speelgoed hoeft u niet mee te nemen, eten en drinken koopt u in ons café" so the "what to bring" question has said alternatives.

## 3. Facts that match real rules

- **8, bus payment:** the national OVpay rule: you check in and out with the same bank card; forgetting to check out costs a fixed fee (about 4 euro on the OVpay pages found on 22 November 2026, which differs per operator and over time) and children up to 3 years travel free (some provinces use other limits). The item uses its own operator "Streekbus Holtrijk" with invented fares (2.50 euro, 5 euro fee, half price for 4 to 11), stated as "bij ons". Sources found by search: connexxion.nl "OVpay: checking in and out with your debit card", 9292.nl OVpay pages. The fee of 5 euro is invented on purpose and differs from the national figure. Reviewer: please open an official page and confirm that nothing in the script claims to be the national rule.
- **6, ventilation:** advice follows the Rijksoverheid page "Hoe kan ik mijn huis ventileren" (rijksoverheid.nl, found by search; not opened in full by me) and landlord leaflets: open windows wide for about ten to fifteen minutes, also while cooking and after showering, dry washing outdoors or in a ventilated room, do not paint over mould. The two-times-a-day rhythm and the sentence "a half hour is not necessary" are the landlord's simplified advice; the sources also stress keeping ventilation grilles open all day, which this short talk omits. The reviewer should open the Rijksoverheid or Milieu Centraal page and judge whether the simplification is acceptable.
- **3, stadspas:** no national rule; prices and the ten working days are invented. **1, energy:** monthly advance and meter readings by app are normal practice; the amounts are invented. **9, 10, 11, 12, 2, 4, 5, 7:** invented organisation rules.

## 4. Questions: keys, evidence and distractors

Every evidence quote is a verbatim substring of `text` (the checker verifies this).

1. **energie-bellen.** q1 quantity, key B 118. A 95 is the old amount; C 23 is the difference. q2 time-place (4), key D "Vanaf één maart". A "vandaag" is when she gives the numbers; B "april" is the start of a lower amount; C "februari" is the last month of the old amount. q3 detail, key A "In de app". B telephone: explicitly refused; C a letter: she received one.
2. **internet-kiezen.** q1 key C 32. A 42 is fast internet; B 40 is the price without contract. q2 (4), key B "Niets". A 40 without contract; C and D are the monthly prices. q3 (4), key D Thursday morning ("tussen acht en twaalf uur"). A, B, C mix the day and the afternoon slot, both said.
3. **stadspas.** q1 key A payslip and identity card. B and C add papers of the children, which she is told she does not need. q2 key C ten working days. A "meteen" is refused; B is the end of validity. q3 (4) key B two euro. A "Niets" is the pass itself; C six euro is the normal price; D "de halve prijs" is the sports lessons.
4. **dienst-eerder.** q1 (4) key C six. A seven is the normal start; B eight is the call-back deadline; D three is the end. q2 key A three. B, C start times. q3 key B safety shoes. A work jacket and pass lie ready; C food is bought in the canteen.
5. **ziekenhuis-lift.** q1 key A lifts at the side entrance. B and C are the two broken entrances. q2 key C second floor. A "beneden" is where blood tests stay; B first floor is where the eye doctor used to be. q3 key B five. A four is the end of blood tests; C seven is the normal closing time.
6. **ventileren.** q1 (4) key B ten minutes twice a day. A, D half an hour is said to be unnecessary; C once a day misses the evening. q2 key A bedroom. B living room and C outside are the recommended places. q3 key C wipe and phone the landlord. A paint is forbidden; B longer ventilation is not the advice.
7. **school-app.** q1 (4) key D before quarter past eight, in the app. A phone only without an app; B notes in the bag no longer exist; C after eight o'clock the teacher no longer reads. q2 key B Monday. A Friday is the hand-in day; C "elke dag" contradicts "voor de hele week". q3 key A within two days. B "diezelfde avond" is excluded by "niet meteen"; C after eight she does not read.
8. **ov-uitchecken.** q1 key B hold the card to the device. A reverses the instruction; C is only for a broken device. q2 key C five euro. A nothing; B is the normal fare. q3 key A children up to four. B pay half; C pay full.
9. **zorg-werkdag.** q1 key C ten to two. A twelve to two and B ten to twelve are cut from the lunch time. q2 key A the lunch. B the course is for new employees and paid as salary; C the bus stops at the door but is not free. q3 key B just walk in. A a diploma is not needed for some jobs; C registration is explicitly not needed.
10. **buurtsuper-sluit.** q1 key A 30 January. B the new shop in two years; C six months of free delivery. q2 key C nine. A eight is the old time; B six is the Kruisstraat closing time on the last day. q3 key B 75 and older. A everyone is not true; C the Dorpsplein shoppers are not mentioned as receivers.
11. **verhuisbus.** q1 key A 49. B 59 Friday to Sunday; C 100 is the kilometres. q2 key B petrol. A boxes are free; C the first hundred kilometres are in the price. q3 key C Saturday and Sunday. A Friday is expensive, not full; B Tuesday is not mentioned as full.
12. **binnenspeeltuin.** q1 (4) key D four euro. A 6.50 is the normal price; B 12 is the party; C "Niets" is children under one. q2 key C nothing. A, B are children's prices. q3 key A socks. B toys are there; C food and drink are bought in the café.
