## Prepared feedback audit

Every sample omits exactly one content goal. Quotes are exact substrings and follow criteria order. Grammar remains correct in the three zinstaak samples so their single omission concerns adequacy; artificial grammar errors were not added because they would introduce a second failed criterion.

- Rooster: sample gives a birthday reason instead of the required course conflict. Its omdat clause is grammatical. Quote 1 is null.
- Geluid: sample asks about opening hours, with correct inversion, and supplies no action against phone calls. Quote 1 is null.
- Buurtfeest: sample covers task with reason, valid 10.00–14.00 availability and relevant experience. It never asks about a preparatory meeting. Quote 4 is null. Typical learner error: “een leuke werk”.
- Verkeerssituatie: sample covers location and 25 children, 08.15–08.30 pressure, obstructed visibility, a parking measure and its mechanism. It makes no timed response request. Quote 5 is null. Typical learner error: “de kinderen dan beter kan”.
- Opleiding: sample gives two distinct work benefits, a specific four-hour afternoon and a permission question. It does not explain work coverage. Quote 3 is null. Typical learner error: “of u geeft toestemming”.
- Reparatie: sample requests opening hours without a solution or deadline. It completes om + te-infinitive correctly. Quote 1 is null.
- Vrijwilligersrooster: sample uses six same-day cancellations out of twenty shifts, four uncovered shifts with the first hour closed, and a two-step replacement method with its advantage. It requests no trial period. Quote 5 is null. Typical learner error: “iedereen krijgen”.

All models fulfil their criteria. Forms provide five or six fields, including four open questions. Personal names are explicitly invented. Table-based tasks explicitly tell learners that the table does not accompany the email. No media is needed.
