# Batch 016: design notes (B1 Spreken, eight korte taken)

Batch 016 contains eight original B1 speaking tasks of the type `kort` (Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I, Spreken I, Deel 1: twenty seconds of speaking time after a spoken cue, no preparation time) as described in `content/blueprint.md` §4.9 and the "Spreken I" paragraph of `docs/research/exam-blueprints-2026-09-10.md`. The batch covers the speech acts of Deel 1 in the spread the brief asked for: ask two questions (×2), describe one picture to someone who cannot see it (×1), state a preference with a reason (×1), give two instructions (×2), react to news (×1), decline an invitation with a reason (×1). Every situation, cue, criterion, sample, quote, model and image brief was written for this project. Official material was used for structure only: the instruction pattern ("U hoort eerst uw collega." followed by the speech act) follows the official form; no official scenario, cue or picture was reused. The sibling batch 017 (middellange taken: missed train, convincing a manager about a course, a bike purchase from a table, reporting a defect from two pictures, two gym subscriptions) shares no subject with this batch.

Level labels are authoring targets (`targetLevelValidated: false`). Nothing here claims official equivalence or a pass prediction. The rater scales (precondition, content, word choice, vocabulary, sentence formation, pronunciation, tempo) live in the `b1-spreken` rubric; the criteria carry only the content checks.

Conventions used in every task:

- Fields: `id`, `level: B1`, `part: speaking`, `exam: nt2-i`, `taskType: kort`, `domain` (werk, educatie, overig), `title`, `status: draft`, `targetLevelValidated: false`, `rubric: b1-spreken`, `speakingSeconds: 20` (no `prepSeconds`, as the checker requires for `kort`), `prompt`, `cue` (`{speaker, role, text}`, role from `config/voices.json`), `images` (at most one: `{brief, alt, kind: drawing, size}`), `criteria` (`[[nl, en], …]`), `sample`, `quotes`, `model`.
- **Three criteria per task**: the speech act and its count are realised by criteria 1 and 2 (question one and question two; instruction one and instruction two; choice and one reason; reaction and one reason; refusal and one reason; layout and at least two colours), and criterion 3 is one content detail that keeps the answer on topic (which course you take, what is wrong with the window, whether cells are still empty, a disadvantage of the other option, one warning or tip, until when, a question or wish, thanks or an alternative).
- Prompts state the situation first, then "U hoort eerst …", then the speech act with its count ("Stel twee vragen: …", "Geef twee instructies", "Zeg wat u liever doet en waarom", "Reageer op het nieuws", "Sla de uitnodiging af en zeg waarom"). Tasks with a picture end with "Gebruik het plaatje."
- Cues are spoken Dutch of 23–30 words that open the floor without giving the answer: `u` from the study adviser, the landlord, the teacher and the child's teacher; `je` from colleagues and the fellow student. The models answer in the same register.
- Models are 38–45 words in three or four sentences of B1 Dutch with subordinate clauses and connectors (zodat, waardoor, omdat, als, voordat, anders, want, dat), indirect questions ("Weet u wanneer …", "vertellen wat je hebt gehoord") and a modal request ("Kunt u haar eraan herinneren"). No prompt or cue sentence is repeated; the models paraphrase the cue.
- Every sample is on topic, fulfils all criteria but one (that quote is `null`) and carries exactly two typical learner errors from the brief's list (verb second violated after a fronted adverbial, a missing article, a wrong perfect auxiliary, plus one congruence error and one lifted prompt clause). Quotes are exact substrings of the sample.
- Pictures: three tasks have one picture each (the describe task, one instruct task, one ask task). No cast member appears in a picture, so no cast traits are needed; the objects are drawn without text, letters, numbers or logos. Cast names in cues (Roos, Modibo, Hasan, meneer De Vries) and in the prompt (Amina) only name the speaker or the child; Thérèse is a §8 name outside the illustration cast.

## 6. Doubts for the reviewer

1. **Criterion 3 as a content detail rather than a count.** In the two ask tasks and the two instruct tasks the count ("twee vragen", "twee instructies") is realised by criteria 1 and 2 and criterion 3 adds a content detail (why you came; what is wrong; a detail to pay attention to; until when). If the reviewer wants the count to be one criterion, criteria 1 and 2 can be merged into "Stel twee vragen: …" and criterion 3 kept, which would leave two criteria and fail the checker's minimum of three; the present shape was chosen for that reason and because it gives the learner per-question feedback.
2. **Model lengths sit high in the range** (38–45 words). Twenty seconds at 2.3–2.5 words per second is 45–50 words, so the models are full but speakable; a reviewer who prefers 30–40 words can drop the last clause of examendatum ("of zit dat bij het cursusgeld"), slaapkamerraam ("of valt dat onder de huur") and koffiemachine ("anders gaat de machine schimmelen") without losing a criterion.
3. **examendatum and the A2 listening item *toets*.** Both involve asking about a test date. The A2 item is receptive, about a class test with a teacher; this one is a productive B1 task at a study adviser about a course exam and its costs, which the brief assigned. If judged too close, the questions can be about the exam location and the registration deadline instead of the date; the criteria change accordingly.
4. **slaapkamerraam and batch 017's defect report.** Both involve something broken, but 017 instructs a colleague how to report a defect at work from two pictures, and this task asks a private landlord two questions about a window. The cue names an invented street ("de Lindenstraat"), allowed by §8; drop it if the reviewer wants no street names in cues.
5. **roosterbord criterion 3 asks for an interpretation** ("Zeg wat de kleuren volgens u betekenen") that the picture cannot confirm. The argument's validity is not judged in the exam, and the colleague on the phone would ask exactly this. If the reviewer wants only observable content, replace it with "Zeg hoe groot het bord is of waar het hangt" and change starter 3 to "Het bord hangt …".
6. **Docent cue register.** The teacher says `u` to an adult student, following batch 009 ("u from a teacher"). Many Dutch teachers of adults say `je`; changing "Die kunt u op papier maken … Wat hebt u liever" to "Die kun je … Wat heb je liever" is a cue-only edit.
7. **medicijn-school domain.** Labelled `overig` (a parent's daily life) rather than `educatie`, which in the NT2 exam means the candidate's own education. Relabelling it `educatie` and open-dag `overig` would keep the 3/3/2 spread only if another task moved too; the brief's counts are met as labelled.
8. **medicijn-school content.** No illness or drug name is given; "een tablet" and "kuur" are generic and the timing (half één, after lunch) is the instruction. If any medication framing is unwanted, the same two-instruction structure works for a diet ("wat Amina wel en niet mag eten") with the same cue.
9. **`size: single` on two pictures.** The brief's record example lists `still | pair | sequence`; `single` is the size the illustrate script uses by default for speaking pictures and exists in `config/illustration.json`. The board uses `still` because it is wide. Remove the `size` keys if the reviewer prefers the default.
10. **No person in the pictures.** The three pictures show objects only, so no cast traits are involved. If the reviewer wants a person for scale or warmth, Modibo (young man, short black hair, light shirt) could stand beside the coffee machine; the board and the window are better without one.
11. **Samples carry two deliberate errors each**, per the brief, unlike the A2 speaking samples of batch 009 (none). The pensioen sample uses a congruence error ("als je pensioen heeft") as its second error because the reaction has no fronted adverbial to break; if the reviewer wants only the three listed error types, "Dan we moeten wel een feestje geven" can replace the question's second clause.
12. **Cast names as cue speakers.** Meneer De Vries (landlord), Roos (colleague), Modibo (new colleague) and Hasan (fellow student) are cast names used for voice and caption only; no picture shows them, so their fixed traits are not in play. Thérèse and the function names (Studieadviseur, Docent, Leerkracht) are outside the cast.
13. **open-dag reason.** The model's reason is Saturday work; a learner may give any reason (family, a shift, a birthday). Criterion 3 accepts either thanks or an alternative; the model does both.
14. **pensioen: "je" to an older colleague.** Thérèse says `je` first, so the learner's `je`/`jij` is natural. A learner who says `u` is not wrong.
15. **Starter merging.** As in batches 008 and 009, the starters stay in the batch file until the focused starter review (section 4).
