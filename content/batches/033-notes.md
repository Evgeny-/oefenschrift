# Batch 033: design notes (KNM, twelve second items for once-covered eindtermen in themes 1, 4, 6, 7 and 8)

Batch 033 contains 12 original KNM items in the format DUO has used since 1 July 2025 (`content/blueprint.md` §4.5): one picture, one factual question of at most twenty words, three parallel options, `taskType: feit`, level A2. Each item gives a SECOND item to an eindterm that the bank covered only once (the launch bank needs every eindterm twice, §4.5), and each states a different fact of that eindterm than the existing item: 1.1.8, 1.2.1 (theme 1, `werk`), 4.1.1, 4.1.4, 4.4.1 (theme 4, `gezondheid`), 6.2.2, 6.5.2 (theme 6, `instanties`), 7.1.4, 7.2.5 (theme 7, `instanties`), 8.1.1, 8.1.3, 8.3.3 (theme 8, `opleiding`) — the same domain mapping as batches 006, 007 and 019.

Which eindtermen were once-covered was established from the catalogue on 11 September 2026 (`content/catalogue.json`, 80 KNM items: 8 study cards without eindterm, 24 + 24 + 12 + 12 `feit` items of batches 006, 007, 018 and 019) and cross-checked against the "Eindterm coverage" table in `content/reviews/006-007-level-check.md` and the coverage paragraph of `content/reviews/019-review.md`: in themes 1, 4, 6, 7 and 8 every covered eindterm had exactly one item; the nine still uncovered ones (1.1.1, 1.1.3, 1.1.6, 4.4.2, 6.1.2, 6.2.4, 7.2.1, 8.1.4, 8.2.3) were left alone as the coordinator asked for once-covered eindtermen. Selection criterion: eindtermen whose indicator list names a second, primary-sourced fact that the existing item does not state in its prompt, options, text or explanation; the chosen twelve are spread over all five themes with at least two per theme and favour the classic KNM facts (medisch beroepsgeheim, kraamzorg, tandarts tot 18, paspoort bij de gemeente, eigen bijdrage advocaat, actief/passief kiesrecht, BSN, schooladvies, vakantie buiten de schoolvakantie, prestatiebeurs, inlichtingenplicht, einde tijdelijk contract).

The eindterm texts were read on 11 September 2026 in the consolidated Regeling inburgering 2021, bijlage 2, on wetten.overheid.nl: `https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0045574/2026-04-18#Bijlage2` (version "geldend van 18-04-2026 t/m heden", fetched with curl and read in full). The verbatim wording of every eindterm and the indicator used is quoted per item in section 3. Every fact was verified by opening the cited page on 11 September 2026 (`sourceReviewedAt`); every URL in the batch file was re-requested at the end of writing and answered 200 at the address recorded (no redirects left in the file). Search snippets were not used as verification. No official exam item was read for content.

Constraints from the coordinator and how they were met:

- **Second item per eindterm, different fact.** Section 1 names the existing item each new one complements and section 3 quotes what that item states; no new key repeats a fact that the existing item states in its prompt, options, text or explanation. The one near-overlap in principle (privacy → BSN) is listed as doubt 1.
- **Not the study cards' facts either.** The eight batch-003 cards were read: inschrijven (register within five days, BSN at registration, used for overheid and zorg), verhuizing, digid, zorgverzekering (basis compulsory, aanvullend voluntary), noodnummer, leerplicht (5–16, ziek melden, verlof for a wedding via the head), stemmen (stempas procedure), discriminatie. None is the key of an item here; the closest neighbours (bsn ↔ inschrijven card, schoolvakantie ↔ leerplicht card, tandarts-kind ↔ zorgverzekering card) are discussed in section 5.
- **Level-check rules.** No key decodable from the prompt's words; no prompt content word (or five-letter stem) that recurs only in the key (script check, section 2); no yes/no key that is the odd polarity out; no key that is the longest option (in every item the key is the shortest or the middle option by character count); no "what should you do" question; keys 4/4/4, no letter twice in a row.
- **Primary sources** with URL, supporting sentence and date on every item (Rijksoverheid, wetten.overheid.nl, Kiesraad, Zorginstituut Nederland, Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, DUO); rules that could move within a year are in section 6.
- **Picture briefs** with cast traits copied verbatim from `config/illustration.json` (eleven cast members used: Hasan, Karim, Fatima ×2, Sabrina, Amina ×3, Modibo, meneer De Vries, Sem, Julio, Roos), "no text" in every brief, Dutch `alt` that does not reveal a key.
- **No invented organisations.** The batch names only real institutions that the items are about (gemeente, UWV, IND, politie, Belastingdienst, DUO, GGD, Raad voor Rechtsbijstand, Juridisch Loket, consultatiebureau, Tweede Kamer, gemeenteraad) and generic settings ("een sportclub", "een winkel"); no company, shop or web address was invented, so no name search was needed.

Level labels are authoring targets (`targetLevelValidated: false`); nothing here claims official equivalence or a pass prediction.

### 1. inlichtingenplicht — 1.1.8 "Is op de hoogte van de belangrijkste rechten en plichten van uitkeringsgerechtigden" (indicator used: "weet dat wijzigingen in de inkomenssituatie van invloed kunnen zijn op uitkeringen")

- Existing item: 006 bijstandsplicht keys the second indicator ("in principe aangeboden werk moet accepteren"); its text and explanation state the duty to accept work and that the gemeente may lower the benefit. Neither mentions reporting changes.
- Source: https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/vraag-en-antwoord/bijstand/wat-zijn-mijn-rechten-en-plichten-in-de-bijstand — "Wat zijn mijn rechten en plichten in de bijstand? | Rijksoverheid.nl" (the same page the 006 item cites, section "Inlichtingenplicht in de bijstand"). Dan moet u dit direct laten weten." — first example in the list: "u gaat geheel of gedeeltelijk werken en ontvangt inkomsten"; consequence: "Geeft u niet alle veranderingen door en krijgt u hierdoor ten onrechte teveel geld? ## 8. Open doubts for the reviewer

1. **bsn (7.2.5) and 019 privacy.** The privacy item's fact card contains the general sentence "Organisaties mogen niet meer gegevens vragen dan nodig." The bsn item is the concrete rule for one datum (which organisations may use the BSN, and that consent does not help) and its key is not stated anywhere in the bank, but the principle overlaps. If the reviewer reads the coordinator's "different fact" more strictly, the reserve for 7.2.5 is the one-month answer deadline named in the 019 notes.
2. **beroepsgeheim (4.1.1), exceptions.** The key "Nee, alleen met haar toestemming" is the eindterm's own rule; the WGBO exceptions (a replacing huisarts, the GGD for a dangerous infectious disease, Veilig Thuis) are in the explanation only. The `sourceUrl` is the law text (BW 7:457) because no Rijksoverheid page states the huisarts-to-employer case in one sentence; the Rijksoverheid and AP pages in the note carry the learner-facing wording.
3. **kraamzorg (4.1.4), "meestal tot en met de achtste dag".** Zorginstituut says "in principe … tot en met dag 8", sometimes 10, by law up to six weeks; the fact card says "meestal". The consultatiebureau distractor: its first home visit (jeugdverpleegkundige) falls in the second week, after the kraamweek, so "de eerste dagen thuis helpen" excludes it, but a reviewer may want "in de eerste week" in the prompt to make the contrast sharper.
4. **tandarts-kind (4.4.1), distractor C.** "Ja, maar alleen de eerste controle per jaar" is an invented cap; it mirrors caps in aanvullende packages and the level check accepted invented rules of this kind (007 geweldloos-opvoeden "een tik"). Swap for "Ja, maar alleen bij een kindertandarts" if the reviewer prefers a body-based distractor.
5. **schooladvies (8.1.1), Amina's age.** The cast entry says "girl of about 10"; groep 8 is 11–12. Batch 019 already put Amina in a lesson about the Grondwet; if the stretch bothers the reviewer, "De zoon van Sabrina zit in groep 8" works with the same options ("De ouders van Amina" → "De ouders zelf") and a picture of Sabrina with a boy of about twelve.
6. **paspoort-aanvragen (6.2.2), gemeente again.** The gemeente is keyed in 019 geboorteaangifte and two cards; here the distractors (politie, IND) are the bodies newcomers actually confuse it with, and the fact card adds the persoonlijk rule and the pasfoto. Comparable (easy end); place it early in the theme-6 drill.
7. **passief-kiesrecht (7.1.4), definitional form.** The prompt is a definition with a persona lead ("Sem leest op school over …"), like 007 bijzondere-school. The fact card's last sentence restricts the Nederlander-and-18 condition to the Tweede Kamer to stay consistent with 019 kiesrecht.
8. **tijdelijk-contract (1.2.1), stilzwijgende verlenging.** If an employee simply keeps working after the end date with the employer's knowledge, the contract is tacitly extended; the persona does not continue, and the page's "eindigt van rechtswege" is the rule tested. The explanation's "van rechtswege" is the page's term, shown after answering.
9. **schoolvakantie (8.1.3), B versus C.** "Nee, … nooit" against "Alleen als …" asks the learner to know that the rule has an exception; that is what the page and the indicator ("vakantietijden bindend") test, but a reviewer may judge the contrast fine for A2.
