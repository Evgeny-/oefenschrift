# Batch 027: design notes (A2 Spreken, twelve tasks)

Batch 027 contains twelve original A2 speaking tasks in the four official types of the DUO "Spreken A2" exam (16 tasks in 35 minutes, four onderdelen of four, no multiple choice since 1 March 2025), three of each, as described in `content/blueprint.md` §4.3 and the "Spreken A2" paragraph of `docs/research/exam-blueprints-2026-09-10.md` §3.1: three `video-answer` tasks (a person asks one two-part question; the learner answers), three `picture-describe` tasks (one picture: describe and give an opinion), three `picture-choose` tasks (two pictures: choose and say why) and three `picture-sequence` tasks (three pictures: narrate what a named person does). Every situation, cue, criterion, sample, quote, model and image brief was written for this project. Official material was used for structure only: the instruction sentences ("U hoort de vraag. Geef antwoord.", "Vertel wat … Vertel ook wat u van … vindt. Gebruik het plaatje.", "… liever …? Vertel ook waarom. Kies een van de plaatjes.", "Kijk naar de plaatjes. Vertel wat … doet. Vertel iets over alle plaatjes.") follow the official patterns as batch 009 fixed them; no official scenario, picture or question was reused. The sibling batch 026 (lunch break, learning words, huisarts appointment; market stall, bus stop in the rain, playground; online vs shop, cycling vs walking, party at home vs restaurant; train ticket machine, library book, cooking for a neighbour) shares no subject with this batch.

Level labels are authoring targets (`targetLevelValidated: false`). Nothing here claims official equivalence or a pass prediction. The rater scales (on-topic precondition, content, word choice, sentence formation, pronunciation) live in the `a2-spreken` rubric; the criteria carry only the content checks.

Conventions, all taken from the integrated batch 009 and the decisions in `content/reviews/009-review.md`:

- Fields: `id`, `level`, `part`, `exam: duo-a2`, `taskType`, `domain`, `title`, `status: draft`, `targetLevelValidated: false`, `rubric: a2-spreken`, `speakingSeconds: 40`, `prepSeconds: 10`, `prompt`, `cue` (video-answer only: `{speaker, role, text}` with a voice role from `config/voices.json`), `images` (`{brief, alt, kind: drawing, size?}`), `criteria` (`[[nl, en], …]`), `sample`, `quotes`, `model`. `size` is `still` for the three video stills, `pair` for the six choice pictures, `sequence` for the nine panels; the three single pictures take the illustrate script's default `single`.
- Three criteria per task, as the 009 review confirmed (answers to doubts 2–4): video answer = part one of the cue, part two, one more detail or reason (this fills the 40 seconds); describe = the scene, at least two of the things asked for, the opinion; choose = the choice, a reason, something about the other picture (the official scoring counts both pictures); sequence = one criterion per picture in order.
- Prompts use the composite video pattern the 009 catalogue items carry ("<situatie>. <Persoon> stelt u een vraag. U hoort de vraag. Geef antwoord.") and the official sentences for the three picture types; the situation comes first; no prompt sentence is longer than 13 words and none has a subordinate clause.
- Cues are spoken Dutch of 20–21 words, each one two-part question with a natural opener: `je` from a colleague, `u` from the volunteer and the adviser. No cue asks for a name, address, date of birth or other identifying data. Non-cast cue speakers are labelled with the article, as the 009 review recommended ("de adviseur").
- Every sample fulfils all criteria but one (that quote is `null`; positions 0/1/2 occur 3/4/5 times), quotes are exact substrings of the sample, and each sample carries exactly two typical A2 learner errors (the author brief asks for one or two; batch 009's samples had none, batch 016 used two): verb second after a fronted adverbial (5), missing article (3), congruence (4), adjective inflection (3), gender (1), verb-cluster or compound order (2), subordinate order in a direct question (1), `moet niet` for `hoef niet` (1), wrong participle (1), missing `te` (1), wrong perfect auxiliary (1), wrong preposition (1). The errors are listed per task in section 2 so that the feedback evaluation can use them.
- Models are 39–44 words of spoken A2 Dutch in five to seven main clauses (average 5.7–8.8 words per sentence, longest 14); subordinate clauses only with `dat`, `als` and one indirect question (`hoeveel geld ik nog heb`), at most one per sentence; present tense throughout in the models (the samples use the perfect twice, both inside deliberate errors: "gekopen", "heeft goed gegaan"); times, days and numbers explicit. No prompt or cue sentence is repeated in a sample or model.
- Cast from `config/illustration.json`, named in the briefs exactly as in the config with the trait string in brackets, so that the author's text and `expandCast` describe the same person: Roos (1), meneer De Vries (1), Hasan (1), Fatima (2: keuken, bus-fiets), Sem (1), Amina (1), Karim (2: kassa, tandartsbezoek), Julio (1), mevrouw Bakker (1), Sabrina (1), Modibo (1). Non-cast characters (the course adviser, the mother and child and the older woman in the waiting room, the customer and cashier at the till, the shopkeeper, the interviewer, the moving friend, the dentist) are described with two or three fixed traits and repeated verbatim where they recur across a pair or a sequence. Sequences show the same cast member with the same traits in all three panels; pairs differ in the thing chosen and name the same setting and props.
- Alt texts are neutral Dutch descriptions; for describe tasks they name the pictured actions and objects, which the 009 review found right for an open task (a screen-reader user needs the content to answer); for choice tasks they describe each option without preferring one.
- No brand names, no real organisations, no real addresses, no text or logos in any picture (sheets, envelopes, banknote, card and van are blank; the bus has no destination sign). No invented company, shop or institution name is used anywhere in the batch (the taalcafé is "het taalcafé van de bibliotheek", the course adviser works at "de school", the interview is "bij een bedrijf"), so the web check the brief asks for had nothing to check; "Ellen" (the teamleider in ziekmelden) is a first name from everyday Dutch, not an organisation.

## 2. Per task: cue casting, the omitted criterion and its errors, how the model meets every criterion

### Video-answer

1. **ziekmelden** (`A2:speaking:batch027-ziekmelden:1`). Prompt: "U bent op uw werk. Een nieuwe collega stelt u een vraag. U hoort de vraag. Geef antwoord." Cue: Roos, a colleague who has worked there for a week, informal `je`, role `f-young` (Roos in the voices file: "student, young colleague"), 20 words in two sentences: "Hé, ik werk hier pas een week. Hoe meld je je hier ziek, en aan wie moet je dat zeggen?" Still: Roos at a small table in a staff room with two coffee cups, turned to the viewer. Criteria: how (phone, e-mail or message), whom, one more detail such as a time. Sample (25 words) omits criterion 1: it names the teamleider and the deadline but never says how the message is given (phone or app). Errors: "zij zit in kantoor" (missing article) and "anders het is te laat" (verb second violated after `anders`). Model (40 words): "Je moet 's ochtends bellen, voor acht uur" (1); "Je belt de teamleider. Dat is Ellen." (2); "voor acht uur", "Een appje is niet genoeg", "Als je weer beter bent, bel je ook even" (3). The rule mirrored (phone before the shift, a message does not count, report recovery) is the common Dutch company rule and the one our own B1 text *verlofregeling* describes; the learner may invent any routine.
2. **taalcafe-oefenen**. Prompt: "U komt voor het eerst in het taalcafé van de bibliotheek. Een vrijwilliger stelt u een vraag. U hoort de vraag. Geef antwoord." Cue: meneer De Vries as a retired volunteer, `u`, role `m-older` (Arjen: "older neighbour, older customer"), 21 words in three sentences: "Welkom bij het taalcafé. Wat wilt u hier graag oefenen: praten, lezen of schrijven? En op welke dag kunt u komen?" The cue lists three options so that an A2 learner has the words for part one. Still: meneer De Vries at a round library table with a thermos jug and cups. Sample (20 words) omits criterion 2: it says what to practise and why, but no day. Errors: "oefenen praten" (verb-cluster order) and "Op mijn werk ik praat alleen Engels" (verb second). Model (39 words): "Ik wil vooral praten oefenen" (1); "Ik kan op dinsdag komen" (2); "want dan ben ik vrij", "Het liefst kom ik 's avonds, na zeven uur", "Maandag en woensdag werk ik tot laat" (3). "Taalcafé" is a generic word used by many libraries and volunteer groups; no organisation is named.
3. **cursuskeuze**. Prompt: "U wilt een avondcursus doen. U praat met de adviseur van de school. Hij stelt u een vraag. U hoort de vraag. Geef antwoord." Cue: the adviser, `u`, role `m-adult` (Robert: "manager, employer, colleague, interviewer"; a male adviser so that not every adviser or teacher in the bank is a woman, as batch 016 did for its teacher), 20 words in three sentences: "Goedenavond. Dit jaar geven we avondcursussen Engels, computer en koken. Welke cursus wilt u doen, en waarom kiest u die?" Still: a non-cast adviser (about 45, short dark hair, glasses, light blue shirt) at a desk with a closed plain folder. Sample (20 words) omits criterion 2: it names the course and when the learner can come, but gives no reason. Errors: "de cursus computer" (compound built as two words) and "Wanneer de cursus begint?" (subordinate order in a direct question). Model (40 words): "Ik wil graag de computercursus doen" (1); "Op mijn werk moet ik veel met de computer werken. Dat vind ik nog moeilijk. Ik wil ook e-mails leren schrijven." (2); "Ik kan op maandag en op donderdag komen", a question about the length (3).

### Picture-describe

4. **wachtkamer**. Prompt: "Hasan zit in de wachtkamer van de huisarts. Vertel wat de mensen doen. Vertel ook wat u van deze wachtkamer vindt. Gebruik het plaatje." Criteria: the scene, at least two things the people do, an opinion about the room. Picture: Hasan on a chair looking at his phone, a mother with a small boy playing with wooden blocks, an older woman with a paper cup of water, a row of chairs and a plant (three nameable actions: op zijn telefoon kijken, spelen, water drinken). Sample (23 words) omits criterion 3: two actions are named but there is no opinion. Errors: "in wachtkamer" (missing article) and "een vrouw drink water" (congruence). Model (44 words): the waiting room at the huisarts (1); Hasan looks at his phone, a child plays with blocks, an older woman drinks a cup of water (2); "Ik vind de wachtkamer fijn. Het is rustig en er is een mooie plant." (3).
5. **keuken**. Prompt: "Fatima kookt thuis met haar kinderen. Het is druk in de keuken. Vertel wat de mensen doen. Vertel ook wat u van samen koken vindt. Gebruik het plaatje." Picture: Fatima stirs a pot on the stove, her son Sem cuts vegetables at the counter, her daughter Amina puts plates on the table (three actions: roeren/koken, snijden, de tafel dekken). Amina's mother is Fatima since batch 017 (eerste-schooldag); Sem as her son is new (doubt 4). Sample (26 words) omits criterion 3: the scene and three actions, no opinion. Errors: "een groot pan" (adjective inflection) and "Amina zetten de borden" (congruence). Model (42 words): a kitchen (1); Fatima stirs in a big pan, Sem cuts vegetables on a board, Amina puts the plates on the table (2); "Ik vind samen koken gezellig. Het gaat sneller en de kinderen leren koken." (3).
6. **kassa**. Prompt: "Karim staat in de rij bij de kassa van de supermarkt. Vertel wat Karim gaat kopen. Vertel ook wat u van wachten in de rij vindt. Gebruik het plaatje." Picture: Karim with a basket holding a loaf of bread, a plain milk carton and bananas; a woman unloading a trolley onto the belt; a cashier at the till (three nameable purchases: brood, melk, bananen). Sample (25 words) omits criterion 2: only the bread is named ("minstens twee" needs two, as the 009 review ruled for gymles, ehbo and kringloop). Errors: "in rij" (missing article) and "hoef ik nooit lang wachten" (missing `te`). Model (40 words): Karim in the supermarket in the queue (1); "brood, melk en bananen" (2); "Ik vind wachten in de rij vervelend. Daarom ga ik vroeg in de ochtend, dan is het rustig." (3).

### Picture-choose

7. **bakkerij**. Prompt: "U gaat werken in een bakkerij. Werkt u liever vroeg in de ochtend of in de avond? Vertel ook waarom. Kies een van de plaatjes." Pair: Julio in an apron slides a tray of rolls into the oven while an orange sun rises in a dark window / Julio in the same apron sweeps the floor beside the empty counter under a moon with the shop lamp on. The sun and the moon carry the time without a clock (a clock face is numbers). Sample (25 words) omits criterion 3: it chooses the morning with a reason and says nothing about the evening picture. Errors: "want dan ik ben 's middags vrij" (verb second after `dan`) and "leuke werk" (adjective inflection before a het-noun). Model (43 words): "Ik kies het tweede plaatje, de avond" (1); early rising is hard, the shop is quiet in the evening, free for a lesson by day (2); "Op het eerste plaatje werkt Julio heel vroeg. Dat is niets voor mij." (3). The sample chooses the other option, which is equally acceptable.
8. **contant-pinnen**. Prompt: "U koopt iets in een winkel. Betaalt u liever contant of met uw pinpas? Vertel ook waarom. Kies een van de plaatjes." Pair: mevrouw Bakker hands coins and a plain banknote to a shopkeeper / holds a bank card against a terminal the same shopkeeper holds out; the same paper bag on the counter in both. Sample (19 words) omits criterion 1: it argues for cash and for the card without ever choosing, the realistic failure that 009's flat-dorp also models; the reason quote (criterion 2) and the sentence about the card (criterion 3) stand without a choice, which gives the feedback its teaching point. Errors: "je nog heeft" (congruence) and "met pinpas ik betaal sneller" (verb second). Model (43 words): "Ik kies het eerste plaatje. Ik betaal liever contant." (1); "Dan zie ik hoeveel geld ik nog heb. Met een pinpas geef je snel te veel uit." (2); "Pinnen, op het tweede plaatje, is wel makkelijk en snel. Maar soms doet de automaat het niet." (3).
9. **bus-fiets**. Prompt: "U volgt een cursus in de stad. Gaat u liever met de bus of met de fiets naar de cursus? Vertel ook waarom. Kies een van de plaatjes." Pair: Fatima at a bus window with a shoulder bag / Fatima cycling along a canal with the same bag; the same weather in both (no rain, which is batch 026's bus-stop scene). Sample (22 words) omits criterion 3: it chooses the bicycle with reasons and ignores the bus. Errors: "ik moet niet betalen" (`moet niet` for `hoef niet`) and "gekopen" (participle). Model (43 words): "Ik kies de bus, het eerste plaatje" (1); evening course in the dark, learning words on the bus (2); "Fietsen, op het tweede plaatje, is gezond en goedkoop. Maar in de regen word ik nat." (3).

### Picture-sequence

10. **sollicitatiegesprek**. Prompt: "Sabrina zoekt een baan. Ze heeft een sollicitatiegesprek. Kijk naar de plaatjes. Vertel wat Sabrina doet. Vertel iets over alle plaatjes." Panels: Sabrina writes a letter at her kitchen table with an envelope beside it; she sits opposite the interviewer (a woman of about 45, short blonde hair, dark blazer) at a desk; she shakes hands with the same interviewer at the open door. Sample (27 words) omits criterion 1: it starts at the interview. Errors: "over de werk" (gender) and "Het gesprek heeft goed gegaan" (wrong perfect auxiliary). Model (44 words): "schrijft Sabrina een sollicitatiebrief" (1); "heeft ze een gesprek met een mevrouw van het bedrijf" (2); "is het gesprek klaar. Ze geven elkaar een hand bij de deur." (3). Panel 1 shows a handwritten letter because a pen and a sheet read at a glance in the flat style, where a laptop screen is a text risk; "Ze schrijft een brief" is the A2 sentence either way (doubt 6).
11. **verhuizen**. Prompt: "Modibo verhuist naar een andere woning. Kijk naar de plaatjes. Vertel wat Modibo doet. Vertel iets over alle plaatjes." Panels: Modibo packs a lamp and plain books into a cardboard box in a room with empty shelves; he carries a box into a white van while a friend (a young woman, long red ponytail, grey hoodie) carries a chair; he sits on a sofa between open boxes in the evening and eats from a plate, the floor lamp on and the window dark. Sample (29 words) omits criterion 2: it jumps from packing to the first evening. Errors: "een andere huis" (adjective inflection before a het-noun) and "In de avond hij zit" (verb second). Model (43 words): "doet Modibo zijn spullen in dozen" (1); "draagt hij een doos naar het busje. Een vriendin helpt hem." (2); "zit hij 's avonds in zijn nieuwe huis. Hij eet iets tussen de dozen." (3).
12. **tandartsbezoek**. Prompt: "Karim heeft kiespijn. Hij gaat naar de tandarts. Kijk naar de plaatjes. Vertel wat Karim doet. Vertel iets over alle plaatjes." Panels: Karim on his sofa with a hand on his cheek and a phone at his ear; Karim in the dentist's chair while the dentist (a woman of about 40, dark hair tied back, red glasses, white coat, blue gloves in this panel only) looks into his mouth with a small mirror; the same dentist hands him a small blank card at the door. Sample (25 words) omits criterion 3: no card, no new appointment. Errors: "Hij bel de tandarts" (congruence) and "op de stoel" (preposition; `in de stoel` at the dentist). Model (44 words): "heeft Karim kiespijn. Hij belt de tandarts." (1); "ligt hij in de stoel bij de tandarts. De tandarts kijkt in zijn mond." (2); "is hij klaar. Hij krijgt een kaartje met een nieuwe afspraak." (3). Nothing graphic: no drill, no blood, no visible tooth; "kiespijn" is an everyday word that the picture explains.

Omitted criteria across the batch: criterion 1 three times (ziekmelden, contant-pinnen, sollicitatiegesprek), criterion 2 four times (taalcafe-oefenen, cursuskeuze, kassa, verhuizen), criterion 3 five times (wachtkamer, keuken, bakkerij, bus-fiets, tandartsbezoek).

## 7. Doubts for the reviewer

1. **No instanties task.** The twelve subjects the coordinator assigned contain none, so the batch covers seven of the eight domains (werk 3, opleiding 2, gezondheid 2, winkels-diensten 2, vervoer 1, wonen-buurt 1, vrije-tijd-familie 1). Batch 009 holds three instanties tasks (gemeente-bellen, paspoort, brief-gemeente), so a form can still be built. If the reviewer wants one here, contant-pinnen can be relabelled only by moving the counter to a gemeente desk (paying for a document), which the author finds less natural than a shop.
2. **Samples carry two learner errors each.** The author brief asks for "one or two typical learner errors"; batch 009's speaking samples had none and its review accepted that (answer 14) because the transcript supplies the errors in speaking practice. This batch follows the brief; the errors are listed in section 2 and are all A2-typical (verb second, articles, congruence, inflection). If the coordinator prefers error-free speaking samples for consistency with 009, removing them changes only `sample` and `quotes`.
3. **ziekmelden asks the learner to describe a workplace rule.** As with 009's bushalte, the learner invents facts (a phone call before eight, the teamleider); any routine satisfies the criteria. The criterion names three options ("bellen, mailen of een bericht sturen") so that a learner who says "een appje sturen" is not marked down for not phoning.
4. **keuken: Fatima's blue coat in the kitchen, and Sem as her son.** The cast rule fixes the coat; the 009 review kept Sem's sweater in the gym and Karim's jacket at the campsite on the same rule, so the brief keeps the exact traits and lets the flat style make the coat read as a blue garment. Amina's mother has been Fatima since 017; Sem as her older brother is a new family link that no other item contradicts (Sem was a pupil in gymles and a supermarket colleague in the 008 note). If the reviewer prefers no new link, "her son Sem" can become "a friend of Amina, Sem" without touching the criteria, sample or model (the model says "Haar zoon Sem" once; "Sem" alone works). If the reviewer also wants to avoid Fatima cooking in both 026 (koken-buurvrouw) and 027, the cast swap is Sabrina (green jumper, indoor clothing) with two non-cast children described in three traits each; prompt, criteria, sample, model and starters then replace the three names with "Sabrina", "haar zoon" and "haar dochter".
5. **bakkerij pictures differ in the activity as well as the time.** The morning picture shows baking, the evening picture sweeping and an empty counter, because that is what the two shifts are; the config's pair rule asks that pictures "differ in the one thing the task asks about". The same bakery, apron and framing are named in both briefs; if a stricter reading is wanted, the evening picture can show Julio at the oven too, with only the window changing.
6. **sollicitatiegesprek panel 1 is a handwritten letter.** Applications are sent by e-mail in practice; a pen and a sheet were chosen because a laptop screen is a text risk in the flat style and "Ze schrijft een brief" is the sentence an A2 learner produces either way. If realism matters more, the brief can become "sits at a laptop and types; a blank screen" with the same alt, criteria and model ("schrijft … een sollicitatiebrief" still holds).
7. **contant-pinnen sample gives no choice** (null for criterion 1), the pattern 009's flat-dorp used and the review accepted. A second batch with the same pattern is deliberate: it is the most common failure in this type.
8. **tandartsbezoek: "kiespijn" in the prompt.** Toothache is the reason for the call; nothing is shown beyond a hand on a cheek and a mirror, and the criteria are three actions. If any health framing is unwanted, "Karim heeft kiespijn" can become "Karim moet naar de tandarts voor controle" and panel 1 loses the hand on the cheek; the model's first sentence then changes to "Op het eerste plaatje belt Karim de tandarts voor een afspraak."
9. **Cue voices.** Two of the three cue speakers are cast members with fixed traits (Roos, meneer De Vries); the adviser is non-cast, like 009's teacher, huisarts and shop employee. Roos's voice role `f-young` is the voice named Roos in `config/voices.json`, a coincidence of names that costs nothing. The adviser is `m-adult` (Robert), the first use of that role in an A2 speaking cue; `f-young` is also new to the A2 cues.
10. **taalcafé wording.** "Taalcafé" is a generic term used by public libraries and volunteer organisations across the Netherlands; the prompt places it "in de bibliotheek" without naming one. If the reviewer wants distance from the 022 listening item, "praatgroep" is the alternative in prompt and cue (word counts stay inside 16–22).
11. **Alt texts of the describe tasks name the actions and objects** the criteria ask for (wachtkamer, keuken, kassa), following the 009 review's reading that a screen-reader user needs the content for an open task. If the coordinator wants alt texts that withhold the answer for these tasks, shorter alts are easy ("Mensen wachten in een wachtkamer.").
12. **Domain labels.** kassa and contant-pinnen are both winkels-diensten; keuken is vrije-tijd-familie rather than wonen-buurt because the prompt is about family cooking; verhuizen is wonen-buurt. Reassigning changes only the counts.
13. **Set assembly.** Together with 026, the two batches give six per type, the same count 009 had; a mock form (4 + 4 + 4 + 4) can be built from 009 alone and a second one from 026 + 027 without shared items, provided the near pairs in section 6 are separated within a form (bakkerij and ochtenddienst; taalcafe-oefenen and the 022 listening item in mixed-part sets).
