- **6 bioscoop-later:** "u hebt geen nieuw kaartje nodig" was added so the distractor "Een nieuw kaartje" is mentioned. The joke is the line about popcorn: "Zo hebt u genoeg te doen."
- **7 zelfscan:** title "Boodschappen doen zonder kassa" (zelfscan is unlisted, not allowed in titles). ## 5. Sources and facts

- **Rookmelder (8):** advice checked against brandweer.nl/onderwerpen/rookmelders on 22 November 2026: at least one on every floor, on the ceiling, in the hallway and landing; not in steam from a cooktop; test once a month with the button; a beep every minute usually means the battery needs replacing. The page gives no battery price, so "ongeveer vijf euro" is the author's estimate for a battery (not tested). The nearby brandweernederland.nl page was not opened; the reviewer should confirm there. The script's "niet in de keuken bij de pannen" follows the steam warning; the page also lists the kitchen area as an extra place, so the reviewer may prefer "niet boven het fornuis".
- **Zelfscan, ID cards, glasses, school hours (7, 10, 2, 3):** no national rule is stated; the details are ordinary practice, invented for an unnamed shop, gemeente, optician and school. Item 10 names only the gemeente and an identity-card appointment, not procedures or fees. Item 3 states no leerplicht rule; "mag beginnen op haar vierde verjaardag" is common school practice.
- **Calendar:** 9, 11 and 12 March 2027 are Tuesday, Thursday and Friday; 5 January 2027 a Tuesday; 6 February 2027 a Saturday; 3 February 2027 a Wednesday.
