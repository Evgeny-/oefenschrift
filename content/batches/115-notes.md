## 2. Per item

1. **kind-ziek-school.** The prompt gives the facts (fever, Thursday and Friday home, back on Monday if better) because the teacher needs them; this is the official "pass on a fact" form. Criterion 4 is the "Bedenk zelf" (model: the missed swimming lesson on Friday). Asks for homework, which differs from `ouderavond-afmelden` (declining an evening). Model adds that the writer will phone on Monday morning if he is still ill. Sample errors: "Maandag komt hij terug als hij beter" (missing "is"), comma splice in the first sentence.
2. **bestelling-kapot.** Prompt says "sommige borden zijn kapot" so the number is the learner's own (criterion 2, Bedenk zelf); the model says two. Criterion 3 asks for a choice (new plates or money back). Differs from `pakket-niet-ontvangen` (nothing arrived). Dates: ordered 24 November 2026, arrived Tuesday 1 December 2026 (a Tuesday, checked). Sample error: "vier bord" (plural).
3. **dienst-ruilen.** The prompt gives the premise and the team-leader rule (it is a fact the colleague must be told); criterion 1 lets the learner pick the dates (model: Saturday 12 and Tuesday 15 December 2026, checked). Criterion 2 is the Bedenk zelf (family day; model: grandmother's birthday). Sample errors: "Wil jij ruil met mij?" (noun for verb), "antwoord voor vrijdag" (voor for vóór, tolerated). The catalogue slug `dienst-ruilen` exists only for an A1 listening item (batch077); the ids differ by level and part.
4. **reparatie-melden.** The prompt does not say "badkamer" or name the faults; the picture and the labels carry them (field 2 asks "In welke kamer is het?"). One picture: a shower head with drops falling and a wall with a cracked white tile below it. The plan wanted the word "tegel"; it is not on the A2 list, so the criteria say "Schrijf dat er een barst in de muur zit" (barst is listed). See Requested words. Distinct from `raam-kapot` and `huismeester-briefje`. Sample error: "in badkamer" (missing article).
5. **computercursus.** The choice of three courses is in the label of the first open field ("e-mail, geld regelen op internet of DigiD"). "Bankieren" was replaced by "geld regelen op internet" as the plan allows (not tested; the plan lists it as a VOCAB check). The learner names one. The slug `computercursus` exists only for an A2 listening item (batch005); the ids differ by part. Sample errors: "cursus e-mail" (missing article); "Ik wil leren." has no object, so criterion 3 is missed.
6. **hulp-op-school.** No school name (every plausible Dutch school name found on the web exists). Fields: name, child's name and group, phone, e-mail (personal data), then three open fields. The third field "Hoe kan de school u het beste bereiken?" asks for a preference (phone or e-mail, or a time), not a repeat of the personal fields. Model: "bellen na vier uur". Sample error: "helpen met sportdag" (missing article).
7. **mijn-stad.** Opening line follows the plan; guiding questions never ask why the writer left. "Woonde u als kind" replaces "opgegroeid" (fine either way). Sample errors: "in de klein dorp".
8. **mijn-feest.** The plan's opening "mijn favoriete feest" is not on the A2 list ("favoriet"), so the opening and title read "mijn leukste feest". Any party counts; no religious practice asked. Sample error: "Wij maak taart" (verb form).
9. **schoonmaker-briefje.** Prompt asks for three things and thanks, so every criterion is in the prompt. The plan's word "schoonmaakster" is unlisted; the prompt says "mevrouw Hind komt bij u schoonmaken" and the title uses her name. Pictures: a window with dirty grey streaks, an open oven with a dark tray, a full laundry basket next to a washing machine (objects only). Sample errors: "schoon maken" (written apart), "wasen" (spelling).
10. **logee-briefje.** Premise adjusted for realism: the plan has Tess staying while you are away, but the spare key on a hook inside the flat cannot be reached by someone who is locked out. Here Tess already sleeps in the flat, the writer leaves early on Saturday, and Tess has no key of her own, so the key on the hook is hers to use when she goes out. The towels and fridge pictures then work as welcome facts. Distinct from `buurman-vakantie` (cat, post, tomatoes) and `oppas-briefje` (pasta, book, teeth). "Logee" is unlisted; the title is "Een briefje voor Tess". Sample error: "bij de haakje".
