## Image notes

One `drawing` per item with `size: "still"`, briefs in English, Dutch alt (all at most 12 words). Every brief ends with "No text, letters, numerals, signs, clock faces or logos." Open tasks: the alt and picture show the goal facts by design. Generate with the shared `stylePrefix`; no media were made.

Rendering risks for the image reviewer:

- `reuzenbeker`: the giver must read clearly as a grandmother (white bun, older face); the mug must not look like a bowl.
- `langesjaal`: the scarf must read as a scarf (fringes), not a towel or blanket.
- `koelkastbal`: an unlabelled glass bottle must read as milk (white contents); the fridge must look almost empty.
- `boekenpan`, `markt-fruit`: exactly two books / four pears, separate and countable; no extra fruit or crates.
- `kantoor-koffie`: coffee vs tea cannot be told apart reliably in a flat drawing; the criterion accepts "thee".
- `samenjas`: two heads, four legs, one coat; redraw if the anatomy is unclear.
- `fiets-mand`: sage green must read as "groen" and not grey; ask for a clearly green frame.
- `miniparaplu`: the tiny umbrella must still read as an umbrella; the bus shelter must be recognisable without signs.

## Doubts for the reviewer

- `fiets-mand` premise: Erik waits for someone he does not know and the learner sees her cycling. Is the scene clear enough without saying where the learner is? An alternative: "Uw vriendin Dana komt op de fiets." with a different caller.
- `kantoor-koffie`: is the boss asking what a new colleague drinks natural enough? The intended reading is a friendly check that he has coffee and is working.
- `tuinbed`: "Waar is ze?" accepts "in bed", so "Ze slaapt in bed" meets both goals in one sentence. This is allowed (one short sentence may meet both).
- `koelkastbal`: "Hij is bijna leeg" in the model uses "hij" for de koelkast; correct but may be harder than repeating the noun.
- The prompt formula: questions (batch plan) instead of commands (blueprint wording). Please confirm.
- The slugs `reuzenbeker`, `boekenpan`, `hondenboek`, `koelkastbal`, `tuinbed` and `stofzuigerdans` no longer describe their scenes; the plan accepts this.
