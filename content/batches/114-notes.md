## 2. Premise changes and why

- **1 proeftijd-gesprek:** "zorgcentrum" (in the situation) instead of "verzorgingshuis"; "proeftijd" avoided. The signing place is only said ("in mijn kantoor"), not asked: the questions test contract length, the signing date and what he brings. The diploma is mentioned ("heb ik al") so that one wrong option (diploma) is said.
- **2 sleutel-kwijt:** "Een tweede sleutel kost tien euro" and "Eén sleutel is genoeg" were added so the price question has a said competing price besides the lock (95). The office is open Monday and Thursday but the key is ready on Thursday, which gives the "when" question a real competitor. "Reservesleutel" appears only in the script (once, explained: a neighbour gives a spare key); the question option says "een sleutel van de buurman vraagt".
- **3 schap-prijs:** "schap" is not on the list, so the script says "het kaartje bij de blikken" and the question asks about "het kaartje". The still shows the setting only (three tins, blank receipt).
- **4 fiets-weggehaald:** "depot" is used once in the script and explained by the next sentence ("Daar staan alle fietsen die wij meenemen"); questions say "daar". "Wegslepen" avoided ("meegenomen"). Not a theft item: Lenka asks "Weet u waar hij is?".
- **5 stage-bezoek:** logboek is explained as "het boek van je stage"; the options say "het boek van zijn stage". The phone number is an invented 0345 number written digit by digit. Not the exam's own example number.
- **6 sollicitatie-terugbellen:** "doordeweekse" is unlisted, so "van maandag tot en met vrijdag". Weekly hours are "tien uur" so that they are not confused with the deadline "twaalf uur". The chain's name is not given (no name to clash with a real firm).
- **7 uitnodiging-etentje:** Lenka is f-adult (the plan line gave "f-adult" to the caller). The restaurant is not named ("we gaan uit eten"), so no invented name is needed. Added "ik ben vrijdag jarig, maar we vieren het op zaterdag" and "kwart over zes" (arrival) next to "half zeven" (dinner) so the time question has said competitors. The bike is "te ver" and it "gaat regenen", so the tram is the single sensible answer. Humour: only the cake line; it needs no cultural knowledge.
- **8 heftruck-veiligheid:** "heftruck" is used once (listed as a compound, passes) and "wagens" otherwise; "hesje" avoided ("gele jas"). Instead of "piept" the script says "maakt een hard geluid". Added "Werkschoenen draagt u natuurlijk al. Een helm is niet nodig." so q2 ("extra dragen") has said wrong options; the pause at half past ten is the third said time for q3.
- **9 pontje:** "pont", "veerboot", "vaart", "varen", "noordkant", "zuidkant" avoided. Banks are "het dorp" and "de stad"; the situation puts the cyclist on the city side. "Het bootje" was kept (one script word, listed). The service hours "van half acht tot half zeven" supply a third said time. "Ticket" is "kaartje", payment "bankpas" (the plan says bank card only; telephone and cash are excluded in the script).
- **10 verhuizen-uitleg:** "borg" avoided ("het geld dat u aan het begin hebt betaald"). "Vier weken" in the plan became "zes weken" so it cannot be confused with "een maand" (the cancellation period). Q2 options: "alle sleutels" is the content; the key "Ook de sleutel van de brievenbus" is a true subset statement, the other two options are false ("alleen de sleutels van het huis"; the bicycle key).
- **11 koor-nieuw-lid:** "koor" once in the script (explained by "zingen" and the situation "een groep die samen zingt"); "zangclub", "repetitie", "bladen" avoided. Added coffee at a quarter to nine, an open evening in March and the June concert as said competing details. "Zingen kan iedereen" closes the item.
- **12 zomerschool:** weeks, hours, age range, places and deadline as planned. Q2 deadline competes with the start and end dates of the summer school; Q3 uses "niet bij de gemeente" as the said competing place. The ages (six to twelve) are in the script and the situation (the son is nine) but no question asks for them, so the plan's "which children" is covered by the situation alone. Doubt for the reviewer: add a question on ages if the batch should test it.

## 3. Facts that match real rules

All organisational rules are invented (contract terms, fees, depot rules, school registration, key costs); none is presented as a national rule. Item 4 reflects normal gemeente practice (bikes in the wrong place get a label and are taken to a depot) without a specific gemeente. Item 10 follows ordinary housing practice (cancel one month before the end, return all keys, deposit returned after inspection); the figures (six weeks) are invented. Item 12 is invented ("de gemeente" is unnamed). No source URL needed.

## 4. Questions: keys, evidence and distractors

Every evidence quote is a verbatim substring of `text` (the checker verifies this).

1. **proeftijd-gesprek.** q1 key B "een jaar"; A six months is when the pay rises; C "voor altijd" is explicitly denied. q2 key A "Op één april"; B today is when they only talk; C after six months is the pay rise. q3 key C bank number and ID copy; A and B contain the diploma, which Floor already has.
2. **sleutel-kwijt.** q1 key A 15 euro; B 10 is the second key; C 95 is the lock. q2 key C Thursday ("De sleutel ligt donderdag klaar"); B Monday: office open but key not ready; A "al morgen" is when she phones the bus company. q3 key B she does not find the keys; A second key is only a price; C the neighbour's key is only a stopgap.
3. **schap-prijs.** q1 key C 1.99 (the card in the shop); A 1.50 is the refund; B 2.49 is the receipt. q2 key B the card; A the receipt is correct; C three tins is correct. q3 (4) key D 1.50; C 50 cent is per tin; A 2.49 and B 1.99 are the two prices.
4. **fiets-weggehaald.** q1 key B Tuesday and Thursday; A includes Monday, the day the bikes were taken; C includes Monday and Thursday (Lenka's day). q2 key A ID and key; B and C add the orange label, which Lenka never saw. q3 key C three months; A two weeks is how long the label hung; B Thursday is the day Lenka comes.
5. **stage-bezoek.** q1 (4) key D Friday at half past ten; A old appointment; B new day, old time; C old day, new time. q2 key B phone the school; A come to the internship; C phone the supervisor, explicitly excluded. q3 key A the logbook ("het boek van zijn stage"); B laptop is "niet nodig"; C work clothes are already worn.
6. **sollicitatie-terugbellen.** q1 key A the shop job is for someone else; B and C belong to the cash-desk job. q2 key C cash desk, weekend; A swaps the days; B swaps the job. q3 key B before Friday at twelve; A is the start date; C is when the job is.
7. **uitnodiging-etentje.** q1 key B half past six; A quarter past six is when guests arrive; C seven o'clock is explicitly not the time. q2 key C tram; A car is expensive to park; B bike is too far. q3 key A before Wednesday; B Friday is the birthday; C Saturday is the dinner.
8. **heftruck-veiligheid.** q1 key B yellow path; A middle road is for trucks; C "naast de wagens" is excluded. q2 key C yellow jacket; A work shoes were already required; B helmet is not needed. q3 key A one o'clock; B half past twelve is hall A; C half past ten is the break.
9. **pontje.** q1 key C quarter past six from the city; A half past six is from the village; B half past seven is the first crossing. q2 key A people and bikes; B cars are excluded; C "alleen mensen" ignores the bikes. q3 key B bank card; A cash and C telephone are excluded.
10. **verhuizen-uitleg.** q1 key A one month before the end ("Een maand eerder"); B last day is when keys are handed in; C six weeks after is the return of the money. q2 key B mailbox key; A "alleen de sleutels van het huis" ignores the extra keys; C a bike key is not required. q3 key C after six weeks; A the day of the appointment and B the last day are earlier steps.
11. **koor-nieuw-lid.** q1 key A eight o'clock; B coffee at quarter to nine; C the end at half past nine. q2 key B eight euro; A nothing is the first month; C ten euro is not mentioned as a price but is a round-number distractor, see doubts. q3 key B June; A March is the open evening; C every Wednesday is the practice.
12. **zomerschool.** q1 key A nine to twelve; B includes the lunch (not part of the lessons); C is the lunch hour only. q2 key A one June; B and C are the start and end of the course. q3 key B at his son's own school; A the website is information only; C the gemeente is explicitly excluded.

Option lengths: key not the longest where it could be avoided; the key is longest only by a few characters in items 2 q2, 9 q1 and 4 q2 (forms such as "Om kwart over zes" force it). The checker raises no warning.

## 10. Doubts for the reviewer

- Item 3: the fix "de bon is goed, maar het kaartje was oud" needs a careful read; a shop employee would say it this way, but the reasoning has three steps (offer ended, label left, price given).
- Item 4: Lenka hears that a notice was hung "twee weken eerder"; the question is not about that, but the number competes with "drie maanden".
- Item 5: the "Je werkkleding heb je al aan" line means Sinan wears work clothes at the garage; the plan said "wear work clothes".
- Item 10: q2 key is a true subset ("Ook de sleutel van de brievenbus"); the reviewer may want it as the "alle sleutels" option.
- Item 11 q2: "Tien euro" is an unmentioned round number (the only unanchored option); anchor it or replace it with "Vijftig cent" if a coffee price is added.
- Item 12: no question on the age range (see section 2).
- Only 2 of 36 keys are D: three-option questions dominate by design.
- Item 7 humour is mild and sits in one sentence; the other eleven items are ordinary.
