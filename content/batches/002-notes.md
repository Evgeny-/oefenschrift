# Batch 002: design notes

Batch 002 contains 24 original pilot exercises. Each language part has three A2 and three B1 items. The material was written for this project and was not adapted from official questions.

## Design aims

- Use varied settings across work, housing, transport, health, shopping, education and neighbourhood life.
- Keep A2 sources brief and explicit. B1 closed items connect details, conditions, paraphrase or simple inference.
- Give each closed item one source-supported answer, with two questions at A2 and three at B1.
- Give each open item three functional requirements. Its sample covers two; `quotes` contains exact substrings for those goals and `null` for the omitted goal. The model covers all three.
- Mark every item `draft` with `targetLevelValidated: false`.

## Limitations

The level labels are authoring targets. Learner trials, psychometric analysis and a full current-exam blueprint audit have not validated them. These items cannot establish official equivalence, readiness or a pass score.

Listening items contain scripts only. Audio needs separate review for delivery, pacing, numbers, negation and neutral emphasis. A script change requires a new audio asset and media review.

Writing and speaking models show possible answers. Other understandable responses may meet the criteria. Feedback should judge each goal independently and allow valid language variation.
