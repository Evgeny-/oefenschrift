Replacement batch for sixteen published A1 listening items after the audit of 28 October 2026 (premises unrealistic for the Netherlands, odd turns, pictures that do not fit). Every item keeps its ID; the reviewer records `replaces`. Base: the current source in `content/batches/077-original.json` and `078-original.json`, full item copied, only the needed fields changed. Speakers, voice roles, domains and slugs are unchanged except the one label in item 15. `text` is rebuilt mechanically as `Speaker: text` lines joined by newlines.

Shared limits (blueprint §4.0, simplified A1 profile): 4–5 turns, at most 12 words per turn and 8 per sentence, 24–40 spoken words, prompt ≤8, options ≤4, explanation ≤14. All figures below are spoken words without labels. New words used from the coordinator's additions: pasfoto, beterschap, bewaren, rit. Numbers are written as words.

Tone: items 5 (dog likes a piece of banana) and 6 (ten eggs, a cake for the whole street) stay lightly humorous; the others are ordinary.

## Doubts for the reviewer

- **gemeente-foto.** "Dan komt u morgen terug" follows the coordinator's decision. A real gemeente may instead ask for a new appointment, which would need words like "nieuwe afspraak maken".
- **potlood-lenen.** The key "Op de tafel." stands for the teacher's "mijn tafel". No other table is mentioned, and the distractors are not tables.
- **bus-station.** The driver's line simplifies contactless check-in ("inchecken" is not on the list) to "U betaalt met uw pinpas".
- **bloem-beker.** Bas's "Hij komt uit mijn tuin." uses "hij" for "de bloem", which is correct but may be a small step for beginners.
- **kind-ziek.** "Ze is vandaag en morgen thuis." is given as information and is not tested.
