## 3. Decisions on the author's doubts (notes §10)

1. **q3 label** → `detail` (E3). The text keeps `opinion` (q4) and `summary` (q7) as its integrative items. If the coordinator wants an `inference` item in this text later, the same fragment supports the official "function of an example" form ("Wat wil Jari duidelijk maken met het voorbeeld van de verkeersregel?" → "Dat een kinderkrant uitlegt wat het nieuws voor de lezer betekent."); not done here because the present question is sound and a new question would be authoring, not repair.
2. **Third `rule-application` in text 2** → keep. q5 requires two rules combined (fridge medicines never in the locker; a mixed order stays together), which is application, not `time-place` retrieval; an instructief text carrying three applied rules matches the official instructief conversations, and q2, q3 and q5 apply three different rules.
3. **q2 boundary** → moved off the boundary (E7) rather than kept or turned into a half-hour key.
4. **Missed-dose hedge** → strengthened at both ends and made part of the task (E8, E9); the with-food shift in q2 was removed on the same principle (E6). The rule as stated (same day: take it; next day: skip; never double; twice daily: skip when the next dose is near; exceptions on the list; phone when in doubt) matches the generic Dutch patient wording and names no medicine. "Op een lege maag" = at least one hour before or two hours after a meal is the standard patient wording (checked on the web, 10 September 2026).
5. **"Apotheker" label** → keep. Function labels are established in the catalogue (Apothekersassistente, Assistente, Presentator); the app shows the label, which tells the learner who speaks; a medication talk with a patient on six medicines is a pharmacist's task and the intro says so. No prompt names "de apotheker".
6. **Register of text 1** → keep *je*: radio presenter and guest, and advice addressed to the listener.
7. **Length** → text 2 is now 888 of 900 words; any later addition needs a cut. Fragment q3 is the longest of the batch at 158 words (65–74 s), inside 50–190 words and the 30–75 s band.
8. **Sign-off and persona prompts** → fine as written.
