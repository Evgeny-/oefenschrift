## Decisions on the author's doubts (notes §8) and the coordinator's questions

1. **scholing (1.1.5) against the eindterm's wording.** Kept as the rule item. Eindterm 1.1.5 ("belang van (bij)scholing voor de kansen op de arbeidsmarkt") has no indicator, and its own statement is the kind of key a learner reaches by common sense; the fact card carries that statement in its first two sentences, and the question tests the one hard, primary-sourced rule about bijscholing (BW 7:611a: required training is free and counts as work time), with the ministry FAQ's own example as the case. Mapping is loose in the way the 007 review accepted for five items; level comparable, no flag. The terminology variant in notes §7 ("Hoe heet een cursus naast je werk?") is decodable and would be easier. A second 1.1.5 item for the launch bank should take the "belang" angle so the pair is not two rule items.
2. **zorgtoeslag (4.4.4) and the digid card.** Kept unchanged. The card states what DigiD is and how to keep it safe (Q: "Waarvoor gebruikt Fatima DigiD?"); this item states that a zorgregeling, the zorgtoeslag, is applied for in Mijn toeslagen with DigiD — the literal text of 4.4.4, which the card never mentions. The overlap is the concept DigiD, which no 4.4.4 item can avoid; the "inloggen" framing keeps the distractors clean (a zorgpas or bankpas is no government login), and reframing it around the channel ("Online, met haar DigiD" against the insurer and the gemeente) would let the laptop in the picture give the key away. Verdict comparable (easy end): place it early in the theme-4 drill; the card is a `leerkaart` and stays out of forms. The second 4.4.4 item for the launch bank should test the help indicator or the "declareren" half.
3. **informatiepunt (6.1.1), hedged key.** Accepted. The hedges are in the fact card ("meestal"), not in the option; BZK says an IDO is "uitgevoerd door de bibliotheek" even where the desk sits in a wijkgebouw, Rijksoverheid names the library twice ("vaak ook … in een openbare bibliotheek", "Hulp in de bibliotheek"), and the zorgtoeslag page lists "een bibliotheek" among the help points. The distractors are wrong disciplines (legal advice, banking), so the hedge makes neither defensible. Yearly re-check that the IDO network exists, as the author asks.
4. **alarmnummer (4.3.2), reachability instead of the core fact.** Kept. The coordinator's constraint was a fact different from the cards, and the card holds "when to call 112 / 0900-8844"; the reachability rule is the page's own headline and a real obstacle. If the coordinator wants the core fact as an exam item, it is the natural second 4.3.2 item (prompt "Welk nummer belt hij?", distractors 0900-8844 and the huisartsenpost); nothing here blocks that.
5. **kwalificatieplicht (8.1.3), "tot zijn achttiende".** Key kept: it is the eindterm's own framing ("kinderen van 5 tot 18 jaar") and a vmbo graduate of 17 needs at least a year for mbo 2; the exception (earlier startkwalificatie) is now in the explanation, where the notes claimed it already was. The alternative key without an age ("Nee, hij moet nog een opleiding volgen") would lose the 18 that the exam asks for.
6. **kiesrecht (7.1.4), no country named.** Agreed that no country is needed; the prompt now states the nationality outright ("geen EU-burger") instead of an origin, which is the thing the rule turns on.
7. **"Alleen …" always a distractor.** Fixed inside this batch: belastingteruggave keys the neutral qualification (edit 2). Across the bank the shape now has five "Alleen …" options with one keyed (006 bijstandsplicht, 019 uitzendbureau, privacy, kwalificatieplicht as distractors), so neither "never the Alleen-option" nor "always the qualified option" pays.
8. **Pictures (bicycle on the road, newborn).** Accepted; both are calm scenes and nobody is hurt in view. See media notes for what to check on the contact sheet.
9. **Concurrent work.** Batch 018 (theme 2) was adopted and integrated while this review ran (catalogue 280 items at the end of the review); no 019 item overlaps with it. The checker was re-run against the current catalogue after that change: no failures, no warnings.
