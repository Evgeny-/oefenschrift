# Content round picture review, part 2, 22 November 2026

All 99 pictures for batches 115–122 and the batch 123 revision were drawn from the reviewed briefs:
- 7 A2 writing picture-note panels (115);
- 21 A2 speaking pictures for 12 tasks (116);
- 71 KNM stills (117–122).

The local model (Z-Image Turbo, 4-bit, 8 steps) drew every picture first. A picture that failed review was drawn again with the next seed. One that failed twice went to the image API (`gpt-image-2.5-flare`, low). The same checks as part 1 apply (`2026-11-22-content-round-media-review.md`):
- house style, with no text, letters, numerals, signs or logos;
- no artefacts at display size;
- named people keep their briefed traits across panels;
- speaking pictures show the situation the task describes;
- KNM stills show the setting without revealing or favouring an answer;
- writing panels show every fact their criteria ask the learner to write about.

Every picture was looked at on contact sheets, and doubtful details at full size.
