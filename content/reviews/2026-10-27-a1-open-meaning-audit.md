The repeated writing defect appears in shoe lunch, onion/apple shopping, breakfast cake and the socked classroom chair. These prompts attach “write a message” to scenes that support a nearby spoken exchange or omit the reason a teacher should manage a seat request. Naming a recipient does not establish a practical message. The source-level content goals and models still align, which explains how a review limited to those fields could miss the problem.

Every item below includes a reconstruction of the speaker or writer’s purpose. “Listener” in picture descriptions is the exercise listener or a practice partner. A description task does not require an invented backstory explaining a large cup, a ball in a refrigerator or a shared coat.
