### 3.1 `B1:writing:batch015-avondcursus:1` — revise

**Shape.** Form deelschrijftaak: six `formFields`, three `kind: text` (name, date of birth, current work) and three `kind: open`, the first of them with the official "Geef minimaal twee redenen." The blueprint asks for four or more fields with at least two open; the official form has six open questions, so this one is at the lighter end (three scored fields). Prompt: situation (Boekhouden voor beginners, October to December, one evening a week 19.00–21.30), then "Vul het formulier in. Schrijf bij de open vragen in hele zinnen. Sommige gegevens moet u zelf bedenken." — the official instruction form. The course hours in the prompt are what the third open field refers to.

**Criteria.** The three criteria are the three open fields, in field order, with "Bedenk dit zelf" on the experience field; the English pairs agree. Criterion 3, however, is narrower than the field it scores: the field asks "Wanneer kunt u niet? Geef een reden.", the criterion scores "op welke avond u niet kunt". In the context of "één avond per week" most learners will answer with a weekday evening, but "Wanneer" also admits "in de herfstvakantie" or "de eerste twee weken van november", which answers the form and fails the criterion (rubric 4). The label, not the criterion, should change: the criterion, the sample ("Op dinsdagavond kan ik niet"), the model and the starter ("Op … kan ik niet, omdat …") all already work with the evening reading.

**Sample.** 72 words. Quotes for criteria 2 (two contiguous sentences) and 3 are exact. Criterion 1 is `null`: the sample gives one reason (the till and invoices at work) and "Ik wil dat goed leren." names the aim of any bookkeeping course, not a second reason, so the omission is defensible (see doubt 6). Two errors, both typical: "omdat ik moet op mijn werk steeds vaker de kas en de facturen controleren" (finite verb not at the end of the omdat-clause) and "voor winkel van mijn oom" (missing article, which the official scale counts). Nothing else is wrong.

**Model.** 116 words, six sentences (average 19.3, longest 25), open fields only, in field order. Two reasons marked by "omdat … terwijl …" and "Bovendien …" (1); no course yet but three years of bookkeeping for an uncle's shop plus daily till counts and a weekly sales overview (2); "Op dinsdagavond kan ik niet, omdat ik dan tot negen uur in de winkel werk." plus availability on the other evenings (3). Correct B1 Dutch with subordinate clauses and signal words; no prompt sentence. Dense for a form but inside every rule (see doubt 11).

**Names.** "Regiocollege Oostwaard": "Regio College" is the name of the real ROC of Zaanstreek-Waterland (Zaandam, Purmerend); the one-word spelling plus an invented town reads as a branch of it. Not an identical name, so a recommended rather than required change; it costs two words in the prompt and nothing else (no other field names the college).

**Level.** Comparable with the official form task, at the lighter end: three scored open answers instead of six, each answerable in one or two B1 sentences; the reasons and the experience are invented, the evening is anchored by the prompt.

**Originality.** "A course choice" is on the needed list; the catalogue's course items are a cooking course (A2 batch001), a group change (A2 batch008-groep), the B1 studietijd request and receptive texts. No item registers for a course through a form. Batch 014's `examendatum` is also about a "cursus Boekhouden" (at Cursuscentrum Noord) — a different act (moving an exam), but the same course subject in the same twelve-task form; see section 8.

**Revision requests.**

1. Required (minor) — `formFields[5].label`: replace "Wanneer kunt u niet? Geef een reden." with "Op welke avond van de week kunt u niet? Geef een reden." Why: criterion 3 scores the evening; the label must ask for it.
2. Required (minor), decides doubt 10 — `formFields[2].label`: replace "Huidig werk (functie en werkgever)" with "Huidige functie". Why: the answer box opens with this label on its own line, so the field invites the learner to type an employer's name, which is the one thing §9 keeps out of a task (a real organisation) and which the model never demonstrates ("op mijn werk", "in de winkel"); a name and a date of birth are on every form and trivially invented, an employer is not. The field is not a criterion, so sample, quotes, model and starters are untouched.
3. Recommended — `prompt`: replace "bij Regiocollege Oostwaard" with "bij Volksuniversiteit Oostwaard" (a volksuniversiteit is exactly the institution that runs an evening course "Boekhouden voor beginners"; "Avondschool Oostwaard" is an equally good alternative). Neither name returned a hit on the web.
