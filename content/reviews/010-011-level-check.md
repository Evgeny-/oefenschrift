## Method

1. Read before judging: `content/blueprint.md` sections 2, 4.6, 5 and 10; `docs/research/exam-blueprints-2026-09-10.md` section 3.2 "Lezen I" (with section 4 on scoring and the reading rules in 6.5); `content/reviews/rubric.md` checks 1–2. Not read: `content/reviews/010-review.*`, `content/reviews/011-review.*`, `content/batches/010-notes.md`, `content/batches/011-notes.md`.
2. Yardstick: the official Lezen I text as described in 3.2 — six texts of about 400–700 words, five to seven three-option questions each, texts 1–5 read in full, text 6 a look-up text (regulation, college information, employment conditions) with persona questions such as "Wat moet Stef in totaal betalen?"; each text ends with "Wat is het doel van deze tekst?"; recurring operations: what a person thinks, the function of an example, the sentence that summarises the writer's opinion, applying a rule to a case; 110 minutes for 35 questions, dictionary allowed. `content/exemplars/` (section 10) does not exist, so no exemplar item was available; the description is the reference.
3. Calibration items, lower bound only: `B1:reading:p1:1` (245 words, three questions, no situation line, no purpose question) and `B1:reading:batch002-volkstuin:1` / `batch002-buurtbus:1` (90 and 81 words, three questions each, answers found in one sentence). These are the pre-blueprint short texts; anything at their level is A2-shaped by the blueprint's own account.
4. Heuristics of section 10 computed by script for every text: word count (digit tokens counted; the count without digits is given where it differs), sentence count (headings and interview questions counted as sentences; the body-only average is given in brackets), average and longest sentence, subordinators and signal words, passive forms, tense, numbers. For every question: prompt and option lengths, evidence span, key letter. Evidence quotes were checked verbatim against the text; option lengths were checked for a key that is the longest option by three words or more (none).
5. Judged per item: whether the text is genuinely B1 (connected paragraphs under subheadings, subordinate clauses, reasons, conditions and consequences, paraphrase between question and text) rather than A2 in disguise (short explicit sentences, answers in one sentence) or B2 (abstract argument, rare vocabulary as the obstacle); what each question demands (locate, combine, paraphrase, apply a rule, infer, read an opinion, name the purpose); distractor plausibility; exam likeness of the form (situation line in the "U leest …" pattern, three options, 5–7 questions, purpose question last with options of the form informeren / overtuigen / waarschuwen, look-up text with persona questions).
6. Display facts checked in the app: `app/components/Session.tsx` prints the situation line and then the DUO A2 instruction "Lees eerst de vraag. Lees daarna de tekst." for every reading item, including `exam: nt2-i`; the official Lezen I has no such line (see the set note).
7. Verdict scale: easier / comparable / harder than the official text of the same type. Flags: too easy, too hard, off-level, off-format, none.

### Item notes

- **ploegendienst.** A personeelsblad interview in the official shape: an introduction naming the speaker and her role, then five interviewer questions as subheadings. B1 features throughout: "omdat het lichaam makkelijker went aan later naar bed gaan dan aan eerder naar bed gaan", "terwijl anderen er stil en somber van worden", "alleen als het echt niet anders kan", a vroeger/nu contrast in the preterite. Vocabulary is work vocabulary a B1 learner meets (ploegendienst, toeslag, ziekmeldingen, rustruimte, vervanger); "voorwaarts draaien" is explained by the two sentences before it. q1 asks the reason for a change and is a real paraphrase: the learner must map "went aan later naar bed gaan" onto "past zich makkelijker aan als een dienst later begint" and reject the consequence (fewer sick reports, C) as the cause. q2 needs the whole nachtdienst paragraph (70 words) to rule out the reversed detail (the break is not longer) and the overstatement (over-55s can ask for fewer nights, not none). q3 is the official "Wat vindt X van …?" form, answered from "Volgens mij is dat de middagdienst" against the common opinion stated just before it. q4 applies three rules to a case and needs the shift times from the first answer: the most demanding item in the pair, at the top of the B1 band, comparable to the official rule-to-case items (see the wording note below). q5 asks the function of the vroeger/nu story, the official "what does X want to show" operation; the distractor about workload is a plausible misreading of "liep elke ruil via mijn bureau". q6 purpose: informeren, with a warning distractor that the sleep content makes tempting. Comparable.
- **deelexamens.** An opiniestuk with thesis, argument, worked example, counterarguments answered one by one and a closing appeal, under four subheadings and a byline. This is the persuasive text of the official form and the densest text of the pair: "Hoewel het eerste argument sterk klinkt, klopt het niet", "Het tweede argument is nog zwakker", the figurative "één toets [kan] twee jaar werk doorstrepen". It stays B1 because the argument is concrete (a teacher, one student, a half-year wait, a practical test) and every less common word (pleiten, afhaken, uiteenvallen, examencommissie) sits in a sentence that explains it; there is no abstract or theoretical passage. q1 paraphrases "toetst de stof terwijl die nog vers is" as "voordat cursisten die weer vergeten zijn" over a four-sentence span. q2 is the official example-function item (Modibo); the resit distractor is a fair trap for a learner who stays at the detail level. q3 requires following the writer's rebuttal, not the opponents' claim. q4 is the official "Welke zin vat de mening van de schrijver het beste samen?" item. q5 requires reading "Dat moet andersom" against the current situation; the abolish distractor catches overreading. q6 purpose: overtuigen. Comparable, upper end; the natural last text of a drill sequence.
- **busabonnement.** The look-up text: six numbered sections of terms with prices, times, zones and deadlines, exactly the "regulation, employment conditions, terms" type the official form places sixth, and the situation line says the reader is looking something up. The rules use the conditional register of Dutch terms ("Reist u … vóór 9.00 uur? Dan betaalt u de hele rit …", "Hebt u een jaar vooruitbetaald? Dan …") and exceptions ("ook het deel na 9.00 uur", "Op het Stadsabonnement en het Regioabonnement krijgen 65-plussers geen korting"). Every question is a persona scanning question as required: q1 combines two figures from two sections (64,00 + 12,50; the 108,50 distractor is the Regio price plus the card); q2 applies the before-9.00 rule against the intuitive "only the part before nine"; q3 is a mild inference from "blijft de kaart geldig, ook in handen van een ander"; q4 is date reasoning around the 20th; q5 is a two-step rule (she needs a Stadsabonnement because the Dalurenabonnement does not run before 9.00, and on that abonnement 65-plussers get nothing); q6 sets the customer-service hours against the persona's working hours and separates the phone line from the servicebalie; q7 purpose: uitleggen. The blueprint's look-up requirements (one quantity item combining two figures, two rule-application items, purpose last) are met with room to spare. Comparable.
- **deeltijdopleiding.** A ROC information page under seven subheadings, descriptive and procedural: what happens between application and first lesson. B1 features: inverted conditionals ("Hebt u geen baan in het vak, dan …", "Betaalt u zelf, dan …"), zodat- and omdat-clauses, passive ("wordt thuis op de computer gemaakt"). The vocabulary is the densest administrative set in the pair (intakegesprek, niveautest, EVC-procedure, vrijstelling, portfolio, beoordelaar, studiebelasting, leerbedrijf, vooropleiding, cursusgeld, termijnen, leerovereenkomst, inloopspreekuur), but each term is either explained in its own paragraph ("een erkenning van wat u in de praktijk al hebt geleerd") or transparent, and no key hangs on an unexplained word. q1 paraphrases "niemand wordt afgewezen … kan … aanraden" as "niemand valt af … kan … voorstellen" against a good distractor (the test, not the adviser, decides on the module). q2 needs the negation "bepaalt niet of u wordt toegelaten". q3 is a persona rule application (nine years in the field, no diploma → vrijstelling via EVC) that requires locating "korter" for "sneller klaar". q4 (3 + 8–10 = 11–13 hours) and q5 (for whom is the page written) are the two lightest items in the pair: q4 is one addition of two adjacent figures and q5 is answered by the title and first two sentences and overlaps with the purpose question. Both are legitimate official operations ("Voor wie is deze tekst vooral bedoeld?" occurs), so the text is comparable, light end; see the optional note.
- **verlofregeling.** A personnel regulation in six numbered sections: sick reporting, first days, bedrijfsarts, pay and holiday during illness, holiday requests, special leave. The highest body-sentence average of the pair (15.4) comes from the stacked conditions of real regulations ("Begint uw dienst vóór 8.30 uur, dan meldt u zich uiterlijk een uur vóór het begin van uw dienst", "Krijgt u binnen twee weken na uw aanvraag geen antwoord, dan is uw verlof goedgekeurd"); none exceeds 26 words. Vocabulary is the work vocabulary the blueprint asks for (ziek melden, teamleider, bedrijfsarts, aangepast werk, vakantiesaldo, bezetting, bijzonder verlof); "arbodienst" and "wat u mankeert" are the two words not explained, and no key depends on them. q1 applies the exception (shift before 8.30 → one hour before the shift) against the general rule; q2 paraphrases a two-sentence list and separates it from what the bedrijfsarts alone may hear; q3 paraphrases "adviseert het bedrijf over aangepast werk" as "alleen een advies over welk werk mogelijk is"; q4 is a persona date rule (3 March + two weeks); q5 connects section 5's ban with the reason given only in the introduction, a genuine across-paragraph inference; q6 combines two special-leave rules (1 + 1, with 4 + 1 as the trap); q7 purpose: uitleggen. The bereavement lines in section 6 are factual regulation wording, not graphic content. Comparable. Shape note: the text is tagged `voorwaarden` but has the look-up shape (numbered rules, persona questions); it can serve as text 6 of a form as well as text 1–5.
- **huisartsenpost-drukte.** A regional news item with dateline, four subheadings, figures (30 procent, vier op de tien, 25 → 14 minuten) and two quoted voices with different views — the "beschouwend" news text of the official form. Sentences are the longest in the pair (longest 29: a terwijl-clause with a relative clause inside it) but the average stays at 15. Two less common expressions ("hangt … aan de lijn", "kijkt er anders tegenaan") are explained by the sentences around them; "triagelijn" and "physician assistant" are defined in the text. q1 paraphrases two causes across two sentences and rejects the negated "bewust negeren"; q2 is the official example-function item (the mother); q3 and q5 are two "Wat vindt X …" items with opposite views, and q5 requires reconciling Ricardo's own fifty-minute wait with "Toch heeft hij begrip"; q4 separates three measures (triage nurse, physician assistant, campaign) with parallel 12–14-word options; q6 purpose: informeren, with the closing advice as a plausible overtuigen distractor. Comparable, upper end.

## Notes that do not change a verdict

- **ploegendienst q4** can be read two ways. "Julio heeft maandag een middagdienst. Een collega uit een andere ploeg wil ruilen: Julio zou dinsdag de ochtenddienst van die collega overnemen." A learner who reads "ruilen" as giving away the Monday middagdienst in exchange for Tuesday's ochtenddienst has no rest problem and answers A (more than 48 hours' notice), which the option text makes true. The intended reading (Julio keeps Monday's middagdienst, which ends at 22.00, and would start at 6.00 on Tuesday) is the more likely one but not the only one. Replacement prompt that closes the second reading: "Het is vrijdag. Julio draait volgende week middagdiensten. Een collega uit een andere ploeg wil ruilen: Julio neemt dinsdag de ochtenddienst van die collega over, en de collega neemt Julio's middagdienst van dinsdag. Keurt Farah deze ruil goed?" Key, evidence and explanation stay as they are (Monday 22.00 to Tuesday 6.00 is eight hours). Level unchanged: it remains the hardest item of the pair and a fair one.
- **busabonnement q5 option B** "Nee, de korting geldt alleen voor het Dalurenabonnement." is true for mevrouw Bakker but not for students; a literal reader may hesitate. "Nee, 65-plussers krijgen alleen korting op het Dalurenabonnement." says the same without the side reading and keeps the option at eight words. Optional.
- **deeltijdopleiding q5** ("Voor wie is deze pagina vooral geschreven?") is answered by the title and the first sentence and overlaps with q6. It is the only inference item in the text, so it should stay unless a stronger one replaces it; a descriptive page offers little to infer, and the text carries its B1 weight in q1–q3. Optional.
- **huisartsenpost-drukte** has C as key in three of six questions; the rule (no letter above 40 %) applies per batch and per form, and both batches and the pair are balanced, so nothing needs to change. If the coordinator wants every text under 50 % on one letter, moving q4's key to A by reordering its options does it.
- **Purpose options** use "uitleggen" instead of "informeren" in the two regulation texts (busabonnement q7, verlofregeling q7). That is the natural verb for a regulation and still the official pattern.

## Conclusion

### A fair B1 reading pair

All six texts are comparable to the official text of their type; none is flagged. The texts are the length, structure and register the official form uses, the questions demand what the official questions demand (paraphrase across a paragraph, rule to case, function of an example, a person's opinion, the writer's conclusion, the purpose), and the distractors are nearby details, reversed conditions and cause/effect swaps, never trivia. Difficulty differences inside the pair are what a form needs: deeltijdopleiding is the gentlest text, deelexamens and huisartsenpost-drukte the densest, ploegendienst q4 and busabonnement q5 the hardest single items.

### Form assembly

Together the two batches are exactly one Lezen I form: six texts spread two per domain (werk: ploegendienst, verlofregeling; educatie: deelexamens, deeltijdopleiding; overig: busabonnement, huisartsenpost-drukte) and over the four text types (beschouwend ×2, persuasief, instructief ×2, descriptief), with busabonnement as text 6. Two points for the coordinator:

1. The pair carries 38 questions; the official form has 35. A mock built from these six texts either runs at 38 questions in the official 110 minutes (the fairer choice, since the timing per question is then slightly tighter than the exam's) or drops three questions — candidates would be deeltijdopleiding q4, busabonnement q3 and verlofregeling q3, the three lightest items. Not a content change; a set-definition decision.
2. Two texts have the look-up shape (busabonnement, tagged `opzoektekst`; verlofregeling, tagged `voorwaarden`). One form uses one of them as text 6 and reads the other in full; the second mock, when its texts exist, can take the other as its text 6.
3. App: for `exam: nt2-i` reading items the instruction line "Lees eerst de vraag. Lees daarna de tekst." is the DUO A2 wording; the Lezen I candidate reads the text and answers its questions with a dictionary at hand. Omit the line for nt2-i or replace it with a neutral "Lees de tekst en beantwoord de vragen." This is exam likeness of the app, not of the items.

### Order in drills (two texts per drill, section 11)

The existing drills pair ploegendienst + deeltijdopleiding (b1-reading-03), deelexamens + busabonnement (b1-reading-04) and verlofregeling + huisartsenpost-drukte (b1-reading-05). By rising demand the order would be deeltijdopleiding → busabonnement → verlofregeling → ploegendienst → huisartsenpost-drukte → deelexamens; within the existing drills, 03 before 05 before 04 keeps the persuasive text last.
