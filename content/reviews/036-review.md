## 3. Decisions on the author's doubts (notes §10)

2. **Text 1 q3 `inference`** → keep. The official "function of an example" form; the catalogue tags that form `inference` (012-mantelzorg q1, and 037 q4/q7 in the parallel batch), and no single sentence states the key: the listener combines the example, "zo gaat het vaker", the consequence and the rule in the last turn. This also gives the B1 listening bank the `inference` item that 025 lacked.
3. **Text 1 q6 `sequence`** → keep. The key and both distractors order events relative to one another (after this talk, once he has the certificate, after the June talk), which is sequencing; `time-place` would fit a calendar answer.
4. **Text 2 q5 arithmetic** → keep as written (coordinator's decision, confirmed): the speaker computes the variable part and models the same addition for the ten-times case, and "wie niets verandert, betaalt iets meer dan nu" lets a careful listener check the total against three hundred and fifty.

The Tuesday/Thursday split is what makes "één vaste dag in uw straat" meaningful and gives the persona item its third condition; three conditions read in 25 seconds is within the official persona form (025 q3 has three). E3 resolves the only tension the clause created.
6. **Register of text 1** → keep *je*: team leader and employee in a warehouse.
7. **Text type of text 1** → keep `beschouwend`: the talk weighs the past half year and closes on Modibo's reflection; the bank needed a beschouwend or descriptief werk text (025 review §5), and this batch adds one of each.
8. **Meneer De Vries's daughter** → keep; the distractor no longer follows the 025 pattern.
9. **Cross-part echoes** → acceptable, see §5.
10. **Length** → text 2 now 881 of 900 item words (19 headroom), text 1 885 (15); any later addition needs a cut in the same text.
11. **"Presentator" label for the gemeente employee** → keep the label (fixed by the brief); the one prompt that named the speaker now uses the intro's wording (E6).
