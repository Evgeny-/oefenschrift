### 1. `B1:speaking:batch016-examendatum:1` — revise (prompt and criterion 3 wording)

Prompt: situation (avondcursus bij een ROC, exam at the end, appointment with the studieadviseur), "U hoort eerst de studieadviseur.", then "Stel twee vragen: wanneer het examen is en wat het kost." — the official ask form. Cue (26 words, `f-adult`): "Goedemiddag, komt u binnen. U hebt een afspraak gemaakt om iets te vragen over het examen, zag ik. Vertelt u eens, wat wilt u precies weten?" opens the floor without answering anything. Criteria 1 and 2 are the two questions. Sample: "Wanneer het examen is?" (criterion 1 realised, with subordinate order in a direct question: the error is grammatical, the content is there) and "Ik doe de cursus boekhouden bij u op school." (3); no cost question, so `null` for 2 is right; second error "Volgende maand ik heb vakantie". Model (38 words): "Ik volg de avondcursus boekhouden en wil aan het eind examen doen." (3), "Weet u wanneer het eerstvolgende examen is, zodat ik op tijd kan beginnen met leren?" (1), "En wat kost het examen, of zit dat bij het cursusgeld?" (2); `u` throughout; a zodat-clause and an indirect question carry the B1 level.

The one problem is criterion 3, "Zeg kort waarvoor u komt, bijvoorbeeld welke cursus u volgt." Nothing in the prompt asks for it, and as worded it is nearly vacuous: a learner who opens with "Ik wil graag weten wanneer het examen is" has said what she came for. The fix is to name the element in the prompt and tighten the criterion to it: append "Zeg ook welke cursus u volgt." to the prompt and make criterion 3 "Zeg welke cursus u volgt." / "Say which course you are taking." Originality: the A2 listening item *toets* (a pupil asks a teacher about a class test) is receptive and A2; the B1 reading *deeltijdopleiding* mentions a studieadviseur in a text; no productive task asks about an exam date and its cost, and "an exam regulation" is on the §9 needed list.

## Revision requests

Policy applied to the third criterion (the author's doubt 1): the three-criterion shape (act and count in criteria 1 and 2, one content detail in criterion 3) is accepted, as in batch 009. A content detail passes when the prompt or cue asks for it, when the picture exists for it, or when it is the conventional shape of the named act at B1 (a reason with a reaction, thanks or an alternative with a refusal, the problem when calling about a repair). It fails when it is a separate proposition that a learner who does exactly what the exam instruction says can omit without fault: then the prompt must ask for it, and the criterion must be worded so that the sample's `null` is reliable.
