## 3. Decisions on the author's doubts (notes §7)

1. **Domain counts 2 / 2 / 4** → accepted as honest tags. None of the four `overig` tasks is a work situation or the candidate's own study; the child's club is daily life, as 028-ophalen (E1 there), 016-medicijn-school and 017-eerste-schooldag. Running total for zinstaken after 014, 028 and 038: werk 8, educatie 6, overig 10; the next zinstaak batch should lean werk (three or four tasks).
2. **The bank's name** → keep generic. No bank is named in subject, salutation or body and no bank rule is claimed; the fictional domain in the `to` address (`klantenservice@zilverdijkbank.nl`) may stay, as the coordinator allowed and as 028 accepted Wilgendam. The web search was repeated on 11 September 2026: no bank or firm named Zilverdijk or Zilverdijkbank (only a silver shop "Zilverbank" and a mosaic bench of that name in Huissen).
3. **"dus" allowing two orders** → accepted as the hoofdzin trigger; a lead-in that allows two correct orders tests the real rule (main-clause order, never verb-final) better than "en" would. The criterion now states the second-position rule with the three correct orders as examples (E1) instead of naming two positions, so "dus nu kan ik …" also earns the point. Feedback note: verb-final order after "dus" is the omdat-carry-over the task exists for.
4. **"Die dag ___." as the inversion trigger** → accepted, as "Volgens het huurcontract" in 028: a fronted time phrase forces the finite verb next, no correct completion begins with the subject, and it is what a parent writes after naming the date. "Die dag, mijn buurvrouw haalt …" with a comma shows no inversion and earns no grammar point; the feedback should say so (same note as "Helaas," and "Misschien dat").
5. **korfbal next to 028-ophalen** → both stay. Both settings were fixed by the coordinator and both start from a parent who cannot collect the child because of work on a September date, but the acts and structures differ (a request that the child may wait at school, dat-clause, to the teacher; an announcement of who collects him, inversion after a time phrase, to the trainer), as do the child, the sport, the addressee and the register. Rule for set assembly: never in one drill (drills are per batch anyway), and if a mixed or future form ever holds both, at least three positions apart. Note that the body never names korfbal (only "de training" and "de zaal"); the title does, which is fine because titles are not shown in exam mode.
6. **laptop sample** → keep. "Anders wil ik heel graag jou laptop gebruiken." repeats the request after an adverb that demands a consequence; it is the copied-request failure (as 028-dienstruil) and earns 0 for adequacy, which the feedback should name as a repetition rather than as an odd sentence. The fallback ("Anders vind ik het ook gezellig …") is the same failure with less excuse and not needed.
7. **Body length** → keep. 46–56 words in three to five sentences is a short e-mail; receptie's 20-word opening sentence is two coordinated main clauses and its 25-word full model sentence is under the B1 limit of 30 (§10). "en die kon ik niet verzetten" stays: it is what motivates asking a colleague rather than moving the appointment.
8. **certificaat: the reimbursement premise** → keep. The agreement is stated in the e-mail itself ("In maart hebben we afgesproken …"), no cao or legal rule is claimed, and asking when an agreed amount is paid is an ordinary work e-mail; 295 euro is a realistic fee for a one-day basic BHV course.
9. **"Is het mogelijk om ___?"** → accepted. It is the standard B1 request formula that takes "om … te" and a new frame next to 014's "lukt het mij niet om" and 028's "een dag vrij nemen om"; 028's "Zou het mogelijk zijn dat" tests a different structure (bijzin). Three "mogelijk" formulae in twenty-four tasks is not a repeated task concept.
10. **inhalen sample** → keep. Asking to postpone the test is a different request from the one the context demands (the writer wants the explanation of the missed lesson, and "Ik kan op woensdag en vrijdag de hele dag" offers days for that), so adequacy is 0 under a marking model that names the make-up request; the feedback should say the task was to ask for the lesson, not to move the test. Unlike the straatverlichting sample (E5) it does not imply the required content, so it is not borderline.
11. **Two consequences, two questions** → accepted; the lead-ins differ in kind (coordinator "dus" versus adverb "Anders"; open versus indirect after "wanneer"), so the structures tested differ, as in 014.
12. **Ricardo, Farah, Roos** → fine; the §8 list is a pool of names, not personas. Hasan (a man in his fifties in the illustration cast, Sem's father) as the neighbour at number 12 contradicts nothing; Roos as a fellow student and Farah as a neighbour are consistent with their earlier roles.
13. **Cross-part echoes** → allowed (parts never share a set) and recorded in section 5; none is a re-skin.
14. **Slugs and concurrent batches** → confirmed: none of the eight slugs exists in any part of the catalogue or in batches 032–037 (KNM, B1 reading, B1 listening); `lantaarnpaal`, `sportclub`, `bezorging`, `pakket`, `burenpakket` and `training` exist in other parts or levels and were rightly avoided; the checker gives no slug warning.
