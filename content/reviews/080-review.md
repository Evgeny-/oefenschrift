## Edits (field, new wording, why)

- Every prompt: the call framing is gone; prompts are now "Kijk naar het plaatje." plus the two questions, with one short situation sentence only in `picknick-park` ("U bent met uw gezin."), `apotheek-tas` ("U wacht in de auto op uw moeder.") and `bij-de-kassa` ("Buurman Wim doet boodschappen."). These sentences identify who "u" or Wim is and supply no picture fact. This also dissolves the thin reasons in `bij-de-kassa` and `docent-bord`.
- Titles: `voetbal-straat` "Op straat", `bakker-brood` "Joost", `picknick-park` "Het gezin", `docent-bord` "De les", `bij-de-kassa` "Bij de kassa". The old titles named the caller or ("Bij de bakker") gave away the place goal.
- `bakker-brood` criterion 1 now accepts "in de keuken" or "bij de bakker".
- `kat-fruitschaal`: criterion 2 now "in het fruit of op de tafel" (decision: "op de tafel" is true of the picture and a natural beginner answer); the model says "in het fruit", matching the criterion verbatim.
- `apotheek-tas` (decision: keep, widen): criterion 1 also accepts "de winkel", since the pharmacy has no sign or cross; model "Mama" became "Mijn moeder"; alt now "Een vrouw komt uit de apotheek. …". Media review must check the medicine boxes read.
- `ballonnen` and `halte-regen` models: "al" dropped; the rain model no longer says "neem een paraplu mee" (it addressed a caller; now 6 words).
- Starters: third starters that addressed a caller ("Hallo oma, …", "Hoi opa, …", "Femke, …", "Tariq, …", "Hallo, …") are neutral; "Er zijn al …" became "Er zijn …"; "Mama is …" became "Mijn moeder is …".
