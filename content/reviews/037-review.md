## 3. Decisions on the author's doubts (notes §10) and the coordinator's questions

Wat doet Julio?" → "Hij werkt door") is a good listening item and is noted here as a ready extra or replacement for a later revision; the fragment supports it without change.
2. **q5 `opinion` → `detail`** (E4).
3. **q7 `inference` → keep**; "beginner" is not said and the "function of a comparison" form is the established `inference` shape (036-functioneringsgesprek q3, 012-mantelzorg q1); the key reworded (E7).
4. **Scaffold fall** → kept as his motive, softened and non-graphic (E10).
5. **One school day and two company days** → keep; presented as an arrangement with the school, near the minimum of practical hours for a work-based mbo route, and needed for q6 B; the sentence split (E5) makes "vier dagen" audible.
6. **Five-year helmet figure** → keep without a hedge: stated as this site's rule, no option depends on it.
7. **Seven-minute bound** → keep the length. At the author's slow estimate both texts touch seven minutes, but the measured rates of the generated B1 clips put text 1 at about 6.3 minutes (Hanneke is the slowest voice, about 2.4 words/s with the presenter) and text 2 at about 5.5; no cut needed. Headroom under the 900-word cap is 39 and 27 words.
8. **"mbo" spoken as letters** → written "m-b-o" in the intro and in both turns (E1–E3). The round trip strips hyphens and compares characters with spaces removed, so a transcription "mbo" or "MBO" matches "m b o" with zero error; uppercase abbreviations (GGD, EHBO) have passed the round trip before. Listen once to the intro and the q1/q5 clips to confirm the letters are spoken.
