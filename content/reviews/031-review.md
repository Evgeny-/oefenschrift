4. **Prompt length.** Trimmed where a fact could leave without touching a scored element: cursus-engels 63 (the certificate wish is in the table), afvalcontainers 61 (turn-taking unscored), werktelefoon 59 (the budget moved into the table caption). No cue was added, so the stimulus counts are unchanged.
5. **Sample lengths 46–58.** Accepted; each sample must show three met criteria and stays shorter than its model.
6. **Cue lengths.** Trimmed to 29 and 29 (edits 9–10); nothing the criteria need was lost.
7. **Colour scheme and the pair.** Grey/green/blue is the common scheme and no municipality is named; but the prompt fixes only the grey container, so what the green and blue take is no longer scored (edit 1). The sample and model may still say it (a plausible addition the feedback must not require). 8. **Sample and model choose different courses.** Accepted, and the same for either subscription in werktelefoon: the criteria score the recommendation, two table points, the link to her situation and the drawback, not the choice; validity is not judged in the exam. The feedback must accept both; the starters are neutral.
