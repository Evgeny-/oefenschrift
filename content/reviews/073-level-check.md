### A1:speaking:batch073-reuzenbeker:1: pass

The prompt supplies the holding context, so repeating `in zijn handen` is unnecessary. The learner need not name a handle, compare measurements or explain the cup's size. There is one central object and no choice among similar cups.

The sample `Modibo heeft beker` identifies the object despite its missing article. It says nothing about size, so the second quote is correctly null and the remaining two quotes occur verbatim. Both content starters and the generic language starter leave the completion open. The brief specifies a drinking rim and single handle, and the alt calls it a large cup. A rendered object that reads as a bucket needs a media repair before delivery.

The alt explicitly gives the same colour and size. Final art must preserve a recognisable blue umbrella at exercise size.
