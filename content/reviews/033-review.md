## Decisions on the author's doubts (notes §8) and the coordinator's questions

1. **bsn (7.2.5) and 019 privacy.** Kept. The privacy item keys inzage and correctie (the second indicator of 7.2.5); its card's "Organisaties mogen niet meer gegevens vragen dan nodig" is the principle. The bsn item keys the first indicator with a concrete rule — the BSN may be used outside government only where a law says so, a sports club may not, and consent does not help — which no card, no 006/007/018/019 item and no 032 item states. Different fact; the overlap is the principle, which every 7.2.5 item shares. The one-month reserve is not needed.
2. **beroepsgeheim (4.1.1) without WGBO exceptions.** Card kept simple and unhedged: the evidence sentence is the eindterm's own rule ("informatie alleen na toestemming van de cliënt met anderen gedeeld mag worden"), and for the case the key covers — the employer — the law has no exception: BW 7:457 lid 2 admits only those involved in the treatment and a replacement, the Rijksoverheid exceptions (waarnemend huisarts, GGD, Veilig Thuis, a deskundigenoordeel by bedrijfsarts/UWV) never reach the employer, and the AP page says even the bedrijfsarts may not pass medical details to the employer. The GGD exception stays in the explanation. The law text as `sourceUrl` is accepted (batches 018 and 019 cited wetten.overheid.nl the same way), with the learner-facing Rijksoverheid and AP pages in the note.
3. **kraamzorg (4.1.4), "meestal".** Kept: Zorginstituut says "in principe … tot en met dag 8", sometimes 10, by law up to six weeks; "meestal" is the honest rendering. The prompt's "de eerste dagen" is kept: the consultatiebureau's first home visit falls in the second week, and the hielprik and gehoortest of the first week (sometimes done by a JGZ nurse) are screenings, not the daily care the prompt asks about, so C stays clearly false.
4. **tandarts-kind (4.4.1), invented distractor.** Replaced, as the coordinator asked, with one the cited page offers: the basispakket table gives the row Tandarts, kinderen tot 18 jaar, with eigen risico "Nee", and the eigen-risico page says "Ook betaalt u voor de mondzorg voor kinderen geen eigen risico." The new C ("Ja, maar het gaat wel van het eigen risico af.") is the adult rule misapplied and is false by the card's own second sentence. The author's alternative ("alleen bij een kindertandarts") would have been another invented body. Polarity and length balance unchanged.
5. **schooladvies (8.1.1), Amina's age.** The age is off: the cast says "girl of about 10", this batch's tandarts item says "Amina is 10 jaar", and groep 8 pupils are 11–12. The alternative persona is used, but with an existing cast link rather than a new one: Karim already has a school-age son (007 medezeggenschapsraad, "de school van zijn zoon"), so "De zoon van Karim zit in groep 8" adds nothing to the family map. The boy is not a cast member; his traits in the brief ("boy of about twelve with short black hair") should be reused verbatim if he ever recurs.
6. **paspoort-aanvragen (6.2.2), gemeente again.** Accepted as comparable (easy end); the distractors are the right confusions. Place it first in the theme-6 drill.
7. **passief-kiesrecht (7.1.4), definitional form.** Accepted; the card no longer carries the Tweede Kamer conditions, so there is nothing left to reconcile with 019 kiesrecht.
8. **tijdelijk-contract (1.2.1), stilzwijgende verlenging.** Accepted: the prompt asks what happens on the end date; tacit continuation is a different case and would not make A true ("tot Karim zelf opzegt" is the opzegtermijn belief, and a tacitly continued contract runs for its old term, not until notice).
9. **schoolvakantie (8.1.3), B versus C.** Kept; that the rule has an exception is the indicator's point ("vakantietijden bindend"). The key is reworded to the page's "geen enkele schoolvakantie" (edit 11).
