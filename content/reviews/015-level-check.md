## Method

1. Read before judging: `content/blueprint.md` sections 2, 4.8, 5 and 10; `docs/research/exam-blueprints-2026-09-10.md` section 3.2 "Schrijven I" (with section 4 on scoring and the writing rules in 6.5); `content/reviews/rubric.md` checks 1–2. Not read: `content/reviews/015-review.*` and `content/batches/015-notes.md`.
2. Yardstick: the official tasks as described in 3.2 — deelschrijftaak (tasks 9–10): fill a form with six open questions ("Geef minimaal twee redenen") or complete an e-mail using all the pictures; korte schrijftaak (tasks 11–12): an e-mail with five or six bullet requirements, often built on a table or pictures ("De tabel komt niet bij uw e-mail …"), with a stated goal such as convincing a shop or a fellow student. Both scored on adequaatheid 0–3 (0 stops scoring; a misread stimulus caps it at 2), grammatica 0–2, spelling, samenhang and woordgebruik 0–1; copied prompt sentences earn nothing; a missing article counts, a gender error does not; word order must be correct. Blueprint targets: deelschrijftaak model 50–150 words, formFields four or more with at least two open, 3–6 criteria; korte schrijftaak five or six criteria, a table or images, a goal, the "not part of the e-mail" line, model 120–180 words. `content/exemplars/` (section 10) does not exist, so no exemplar task was available; the description is the reference.
3. Calibration items, lower bound only: `B1:writing:batch002-rooster:1`, `batch002-geluid:1`, `batch003-opleiding:1`, `batch003-reparatie:1` — the pre-blueprint "message" format: one- or two-sentence prompt, three bullets, no header, models of 22–32 words. The research document rates them smaller than the official A2 e-mail, so they mark the floor, not the target.
4. Heuristics of section 10 computed by script for every prompt, model and sample: words (digit tokens counted; the count without digits given where it differs), sentences, average and longest sentence, subordinators and signal words, tense, numbers. For each task: prompt level, what the criteria demand (B1 speech acts: give reasons, describe experience, narrate past events, complain with specifics, propose with figures, request with a deadline), whether every criterion can be met from the stimulus or by marked invention, whether the model meets every criterion in the right register and length, scaffold and register, table or form completeness, exam likeness of the instruction wording.
5. The three drawings of gemiste-werkdag were looked at in `content/images-source/` (e80fe34aa4785574, 892a97c0b918807a, a30fb7b7fa7f4a8d), since in a picture e-mail the pictures carry the task.
6. Display facts that shape the verdicts, checked in the app. `app/components/OpenExercise.tsx` prints the criteria under the prompt before writing for form tasks and korte schrijftaken, and hides them before writing for a `deelschrijftaak` with images (the rule the 008 level check asked for is in place), so the picture task keeps its lexical step. The feedback judge (`server/services.ts`) receives the prompt, the Dutch criteria, the table and the form labels, so the criteria carry the scoring content. Sentence starters (`content/hints/sentence-starters.json`) are a collapsed, opt-in aid and were left out of the verdicts, with one note below.
7. Verdict scale: easier / comparable / harder than the official task of the same type. Flags: too easy, too hard, off-level, off-format, none.

### Item notes

- **avondcursus.** Prompt: six short sentences that give the course, its period and evening hours and say "Sommige gegevens moet u zelf bedenken." The form has three data fields (name, date of birth, current job) and three open questions, each a B1 speech act: two reasons (argue), experience with the subject (describe, invented), an evening you cannot attend with a reason (state a constraint and justify it). The official form has six open questions; this one has three, so the adequacy demand is spread over fewer elements, but each element asks more ("minimaal twee redenen", experience plus its relevance, an evening plus a reason), and the model needed 116 words in six sentences (average 19, longest 25) to answer them — the same production as a six-question form with shorter answers. Model: omdat with correct verb-final order twice, "terwijl ik dat nooit heb geleerd", bovendien, dus, perfect tense for the experience; every criterion met. Comparable, light end on count; see the optional fourth open question below.
- **gemiste-werkdag.** The picture e-mail of the official form. Prompt: the setting (groothandel in kantoorartikelen), the addressee's situation (Julio was ill), the task ("u vertelt wat er vandaag op het werk is gebeurd. Kijk naar de drie plaatjes. Maak de e-mail af. Gebruik alle plaatjes."), plus the question to Julio. The three drawings each show one readable event with the same character (green jumper, long dark hair) in all three and no text: a technician kneeling at an open coffee machine with a screwdriver and loose parts while Sabrina holds an empty cup; Sabrina at a whiteboard with four coloured blocks and arrows in front of four colleagues, one raising a hand; Sabrina shaking hands with a young man holding a clipboard while gesturing along warehouse shelves. Pictures 1 and 2 have one reading each (broken machine repaired; a meeting or presentation). Picture 3 reads most naturally as a new colleague being welcomed and shown the warehouse, which is what the criterion asks; a learner could also read "a visitor" — see the note. The criteria demand narrating three past events (perfect or preterite), inventing the meeting's topic and asking a question: the B1 demand of the official task. The criteria are hidden before writing, so the learner has to find koffieapparaat, monteur, repareren, overleg, nieuwe collega, magazijn, rondleiden themselves — where the woordgebruik point is earned. Model: 132 words, ten sentences, "zodat niemand koffie kon maken", a relative clause "die het apparaat binnen een uur heeft gerepareerd", "legde uit dat … omdat … voordat …", "Omdat hij in het magazijn gaat werken, heeft …", then the question and a reason to come back; je/jij held throughout. Comparable.
- **kledingkast.** The official complaint korte schrijftaak: an order table (number, article, price and delivery cost, order date, promised date, actual date, damage) and five requirements — what and when ordered, two problems, what you already did (invented), what you want and by when, a polite but firm request for a reply. The prompt states the goal ("u wilt klagen en u wilt dat de winkel het probleem oplost") and the table line. Everything the learner needs is in the table (five days late: 4 versus 9 September; a scratched and dented door) except the invented element, which the prompt seeds ("U hebt al contact gehad met de klantenservice"). Model: 178 words in eleven sentences, "Ten eerste … Ten tweede …", "terwijl ik daarvoor twee dagen vrij had genomen", passive "zou … worden bezorgd", preterite bleek / beloofde, a deadline with a consequence ("Als ik dan nog niets heb gehoord, annuleer ik de bestelling"); u held throughout; every criterion met. The dates in the table are consistent with the 2026 calendar (25 August a Tuesday, 4 September a Friday, 9 September a Wednesday, 18 September a Friday). Comparable.
- **pauzerooster.** The proposal korte schrijftaak: a rota table with a caption that gives the shift and the canteen's twenty seats, three teams with head counts and two breaks each, and six requirements — the problem, two reasons it matters, a concrete new rota with times, one advantage for the company, a meeting request with a proposed moment, a fitting close. Six is the official maximum and the sixth (a closing line) is light, as the official "Sluit af" bullets are. The learner has to design a rota; the sample shows the simple solution (shift team B by fifteen and thirty minutes, team C after it), so the arithmetic is small. ## Notes that do not change a verdict

- **avondcursus, count of open questions.** The official form has six open questions; this form has three. The blueprint's minimum (four fields, two open) is met and the production matches, so no flag. If the coordinator wants the form closer to the official count, a fourth open field of low load fits without pushing the model past 150 words: field label "Wat wilt u aan het einde van de cursus kunnen? Noem één voorbeeld." (`kind: open`); criterion "Schrijf wat u aan het einde van de cursus wilt kunnen; noem één voorbeeld." / "Say what you want to be able to do by the end of the course; give one example."; model sentence to append: "Aan het einde van de cursus wil ik zelf de maandcijfers van een winkel kunnen bijhouden, zonder hulp van een boekhouder." (model 137 words). A change of criteria re-opens the starters review (section 12 step 8).
- **gemiste-werkdag, picture 3.** The handshake, the clipboard and the gesture along the shelves make "a new colleague being shown the warehouse" the likely reading, but "a customer or visitor" is possible, and criterion 3 names "een nieuwe collega". The official marking model tolerates any plausible reading of a picture as long as the intention is clear; the feedback judge works from the criterion text. Two ways to keep that tolerance without giving the words away: (a) leave the picture and add to criterion 3 the English gloss the judge sees anyway, so that "een nieuwe medewerker / iemand die pas begonnen is" count — the Dutch criterion could read "Schrijf dat er iemand nieuw is begonnen (een nieuwe collega) en dat iemand hem het magazijn heeft laten zien."; or (b) regenerate picture 3 with a stronger first-day cue, for example Modibo carrying a small box of personal things or wearing a visitor-style badge on a lanyard, keeping the handshake and the shelves. Option (a) is cheaper and enough.
- **gemiste-werkdag, sentence starters.** The starters for this task read "Vanmorgen was het koffieapparaat …", "Om … uur hadden we een teamoverleg over …", "Er is een nieuwe collega begonnen, die …" — the picture words that the hidden criteria protect. They are collapsed and opt-in, so they were left out of the verdict as in the 008 check, but for a picture task an opened starter list removes the lexical step. Two fixes: hide starters before writing for a `deelschrijftaak` with images, the same rule as for its criteria (app), or make the starters neutral (content): "Vanmorgen was … kapot, maar gelukkig …", "Om … uur hadden we … over …", "Verder heeft Sabrina vanmiddag … laten zien, omdat …", "Laat je even weten hoe het gaat en wanneer …?". Either way they should stay hidden in mock mode.
- **gemiste-werkdag, e-mail start.** The official "maak de e-mail af" task gives the opening lines of the e-mail and the candidate continues; here the scaffold gives header, salutation and closing and the learner writes the whole body. The blueprint's field set for a picture deelschrijftaak does not include body lines, so the item conforms; if a later revision adds an opening line, "Je hebt vandaag heel wat gemist!" is neutral and does not name a picture.
- **pauzerooster, criteria 2 and 4.** Two reasons the problem matters (criterion 2) and one advantage for the company (criterion 4) will often mirror each other in a learner text (lost work time as reason, no lost work time as advantage). That is how the official bullets behave too; the judge should accept an advantage that mirrors a reason when it is phrased as a benefit of the proposal.
- **Samples.** Each sample is a plausible B1 learner text that misses exactly one criterion (the `null` in `quotes`) and contains the error types the official grammar and spelling scales target: main-clause order after omdat ("omdat ik moet op mijn werk …", "omdat de kast is pas … bezorgd", "omdat er zijn maar twintig stoelen"), subordinate order after dat ("Ik denk dat hij is een aardige jongen"), a missing article ("voor winkel van mijn oom", which the official scale counts), participle spelling ("gerepareert", "beschadigt"), a plural ("tien minuut"). They support the level claim and calibrate feedback.
- **Section 10 model lengths.** Both korte schrijftaak models sit at 178 words, the top of the 120–180 band; with six elements and times to state, that is where a complete answer lands. The band is right; authors should not go above it.

## Conclusion

### A fair B1 writing set

All four tasks are comparable to the official task of their type; none is flagged. The two deelschrijftaken are one of each official kind (a form with open questions, a picture e-mail); the two korte schrijftaken have five and six requirements on a table, a stated goal each (klagen, voorstellen), the official "not part of the e-mail" line and models inside 120–180 words. The criteria ask for B1 speech acts — two reasons, described experience, narrated events, two specified problems, a proposal with figures, a request with a deadline — and every criterion can be met from the stimulus or by invention the prompt marks. Registers are held (form; informal collega; formal webwinkel; formal leidinggevende).

### Form assembly

With the eight zinstaken of batch 014, batch 015 completes one Schrijven I form (8 + 2 + 2). The second mock needs another 8 + 2 + 2 with no shared task; for spread, the next korte schrijftaken should take the two goals not yet used (informeren, overtuigen — the research names convincing a fellow student), the next form deelschrijftaak could carry five or six open questions to sit at the official count, and the next picture e-mail could give an opening line. Domains in 015: educatie, werk, overig, werk.

### Order in drills (two tasks of one type per drill, section 11)

- b1-writing-03 (deelschrijftaken): avondcursus first (criteria visible, facts in the prompt), then gemiste-werkdag (criteria hidden, words from the pictures).
- b1-writing-04 (korte schrijftaken): kledingkast first (five requirements, the table supplies the facts), then pauzerooster (six requirements, the learner designs the rota).

### Recommended actions

1. **gemiste-werkdag** (coordinator/app or author): hide starters before writing for a `deelschrijftaak` with images, or replace them with the neutral starters above; optionally widen criterion 3's wording so the judge accepts "iemand nieuw" readings.
2. Optional: a fourth open question for avondcursus (text above), with the starters re-reviewed under section 12 step 8.
3. Nothing else needs to change before these four tasks go into a form.

## Limitations

- No exemplar task exists in the repository, and I have not seen the official Schrijven I tasks themselves; the yardstick is the verified description in `docs/research/exam-blueprints-2026-09-10.md`. In particular the "six open questions" of the official form is taken from that description, not from a form I could inspect.
- The heuristics are regex-based approximations; they support the reading, they do not replace it.
- Feedback quality was not tested here; the verdicts assume the judge applies the criteria as written and tolerates natural wording variants.
- One reviewer, one reading; levels remain authoring targets (`targetLevelValidated: false`) until an NT2 teacher or a learner pilot confirms them.
- Invented names (Volksuniversiteit Oostwaard, Kastenzolder, Logistiek Veldwijk) were not web-checked here; section 13 records that two invented names in this batch once belonged to real businesses, so the coordinator's check matters for this batch in particular.
- The first reviewer's notes and the author's notes were not read, as the brief requires; a point they already settled may reappear above.
