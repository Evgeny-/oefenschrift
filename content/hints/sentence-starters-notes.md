# Sentence starters overlay

This overlay covers the 36 writing and speaking exercises in the catalogue snapshot with SHA-256 `52e0e5f6fbd90ffcad09a1f872dee03681e922c184f973e611ce09b96773b666`.

Each item has three short Dutch fragments in criteria order. Every fragment contains an ellipsis and prompts the learner to supply the task-specific fact, reason, date, time, description, consequence or proposal. The fragments were checked against the criteria rather than copied from model answers. They are hints for getting started and are not complete sample responses.

Scope is limited to content metadata. The catalogue and application code are unchanged.
