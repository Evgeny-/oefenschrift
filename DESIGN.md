The wordmark is Oefenschrift set in Nunito 800 as outlines, the O cut in two with the upper 47% and the dot of the i in the accent colour and a hairline of paper along the cut (one pixel at sidebar size; the accent takes less than half because a light shape reads larger than a dark one). The cut is straight; the user tried the icon's 30-degree tilt on the word and preferred it straight (`siteCut` in `fonts.mjs`). It is inline SVG generated into `app/components/logo.ts` by `docs/logos/fonts.mjs`: the ink follows `currentColor` and the accent `--action`, so it works in both themes, and it is capped at 8.5rem wide, a tenth less than the sidebar allows. It links to the start page, and a click on it swings the cut through the O to the icon's 30 degrees, back past straight and down to rest like a wave (720ms, CSS keyframes on the two clip boxes around the O's centre, whose position the generated module carries as `origin`); the motion plays on every click, also when the click leaves another section and the sidebar is remounted with the start page's route element — the click time is kept outside React so the surviving instance plays it — and never on load or hover; reduced motion turns it off. The choice came out of a September 2026 round kept under `docs/logos/`: six concept drafts from the user's picture (the split O won over a coloured second word, an accented In and underlines), `fonts.mjs` with the split O in over a hundred typefaces on a page where the cut angle, share and gap can be changed, and `designs.mjs` with forty-odd other treatments plus combinations of the user's favourites (a tapered pen stroke under schrift, an offset accent shadow, the split O in Lilita One and Fraunces); the pages, the font files and the temporary sidebar switchers that showed all of this on the live site are gone from the app and reproducible from the generators.

The site icon is the wordmark's O on its own, cut at 30 degrees rising to the right, on an ink tile with corners at 22% of the side — accent above, paper below — so it looks the same in every tab bar and in Safari, which cannot use an adaptive SVG (the user chose the tilt and then the dark tile over paper; a bare mark whose ink half follows the colour scheme is `split-30` on the candidates page) — `assets/icons/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48 as PNG entries), `icon-32.png` and a 180px `apple-touch-icon.png` as a full square, because phones round it themselves and paint transparent corners black; `docs/logos/icon.mjs` builds them from the same outlines with its own small rasteriser (sixteen samples per pixel, transparent corners — Quick Look, tried first, flattened the rounded corners onto white and Chrome showed white dots around the tab icon), and `scripts/prepare.ts` copies them to the site root. The same script draws 32 candidates into `docs/logos/oefenschrift-icons.html` — the O alone in several cuts and colourings, on paper, accent and ink tiles, on discs, and as an Oe or lowercase-o monogram — each in a mock browser tab in both themes and at 16, 32 and 48 pixels; `chosen` in the script names the one in use.

Typography settled in the same round: Fira Sans for the text — 400, 600 for interface bold, 700 for emphasis — and Nunito for headings and the wordmark, 700 for sections and 800 for the page title, both loaded from `assets/fonts` and preloaded. The user first wanted Nunito for headings and something else for the text, then a face as soft as Nunito for headings too; twenty-two soft heading faces and twenty-five text faces were compared live (`docs/logos/typefaces.mjs` still builds the comparison stylesheet into the ignored `docs/logos/out/`). Text faces were compared with `font-size-adjust` to Public Sans's x-height rather than by scaling the root, because scaling the root changed every rem measurement and made the page wider under faces with a small x-height. Alternatives the user liked and may return to: Open Sans, Lato and Nunito Sans for the text; Nunito Sans was rejected for headings. With Nunito the heading sizes came down a step — the title 1.625rem instead of 1.875rem, sections 1.0625rem instead of 1.125rem, the start page's hero 2.125rem instead of 2.5rem (1.625rem on phones) — buttons stay in the text face (the primary action briefly took the heading face and the two buttons on the start page disagreed), and row titles in the catalogue and on the start page (Practice set 3, an exercise's title, a level check) are `h3` elements, so they take the heading face at body size. Public Sans, the previous text face, stays in `assets/fonts` only as the generator's reference.
