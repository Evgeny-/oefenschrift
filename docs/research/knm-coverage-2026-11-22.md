# KNM coverage audit, 22 November 2026

A Sonnet audit of the KNM bank against the eindtermen in force since 1 July 2025. It is the basis for KNM batches 108–112 (`docs/briefs/content-round-2026-11-22.md`). It is an editorial coverage count; it makes no claim about difficulty or exam equivalence.

## 0. Summary of decisions

- The arithmetic does not work with 56. The bank has 104 items on 65 of the 78 eindtermen (section 2): **13 eindtermen have no item (the nine noted earlier plus all four of theme 5) and 40 have exactly one item**. Bringing every eindterm to two needs 13 x 2 + 40 x 1 = **66 new items, giving 170** (not 160). Of these 66, 56 are priority P1 and 10 are P2 (second item for an eindterm that is already weakly or closely covered; cut these first). With only the 56 P1 items the bank reaches 160, and **ten eindtermen stay at one item**: 1.1.5, 1.2.3, 4.2.1, 4.4.3, 6.3.1, 6.6.1, 7.1.2, 7.2.7, 7.2.8, 8.3.2.
- Theme sizes cannot be balanced toward 20 each while meeting the floor of two: after 66 items themes are 26, 24, 12, 25, 8, 27, 25, 23. Theme 3 (6 eindtermen) and theme 5 (4 eindtermen) are small by construction. Section 5 lists four optional extra theme-5 items (reserve) if a flatter distribution is wanted.
- Theme 5 is testable and is part of the official eindtermen (section 3). No source I found explains why it was skipped; it is simply not written yet.
- The eindterm list used by the bank is correct: the 2024-15802 text (Regeling inburgering 2021, bijlage 2, geldend 18 April 2026 to date) has 78 numbered eindtermen; the 104 existing items carry only eindterm numbers that exist.

## 3. Theme 5 (Geschiedenis en geografie)

- **It is a real, testable theme.** Bijlage 2 lists four eindtermen (5.1.1, 5.1.2, 5.2.1, 5.2.2). The Rijksoverheid news item of 30 June 2025 says the exam now tests 'wat de holocaust inhoudt' (eindterm 5.1.2), which lives in theme 5. inburgeren.nl lists themes only by example ('wonen', 'werk en inkomen') and gives no list; DUO does not publish a per-theme question count.
- **Why the bank has no theme-5 item: not found.** The batch notes and reviews (006, 019, 033, level checks) only record 'theme 5 has no items' without a reason. No document says it cannot be tested. It was left over as 'not written yet'. My reading: the earlier batches started from practical themes (work, housing, health, institutions) and the 'photo plus fact' format maps well to theme 5 (maps, flag, dikes, Deltawerken, monuments), so there is no format obstacle.
- **Official practice exams.** The two DUO oefenexamens KNM are in an external player linked from [inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/oefenen](https://www.inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/oefenen.jsp); I did not open them in this audit, so I cannot say whether theme 5 appears in them. The September research note recorded 'eight themes' with divider screens, which suggests it does.
- **Image notes.** Real photographs (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY or CC BY-SA, credit recorded) are allowed for real places and objects (Oosterscheldekering, map of the provinces, Dutch flag); drawings for persona scenes. Holocaust and slavery items: no people in distress, monument or neutral calendar picture only.
- **Theme 5 will still be the smallest theme** (8 items for 4 eindtermen, 12 if the optional reserve is used), so a theme-5 drill holds at most 8-12 items.
