Create a fresh design concept and clickable mockups for a free public website where people can prepare for Dutch inburgering exams. Start with A2 and KNM, with room for B1 and B2 later. People should be able to practise without an account and understand their mistakes. Speaking and writing could have optional AI feedback; I have ElevenLabs access for Dutch audio and transcription.

I want a professional, stylish, minimalist service that feels thoughtfully designed. The previous mockup looked obviously AI-generated, which I disliked. Develop your own visual direction. The purpose is to explore how this service could feel and work before we build it.

The project is at the project directory. Original sample exercises are in `pilots/a2-reading-001.json` and `pilots/b1-reading-001.json`, with readable Markdown versions. I reviewed the A2 sample and found its level appropriate. `research/service-plan.md` contains product context, and `research/feasibility.md` explains the data research.

Official references: https://inburgeren.nl/examen-doen/oefenen.jsp and https://oefenexamensnt2.nl/. Local reference files and source URLs are in `reference-private/`, especially `manifest.json` and `duo-practice-links.json`. Those official materials are study references, not cleared for republication. Use the original pilots for mockup content.
