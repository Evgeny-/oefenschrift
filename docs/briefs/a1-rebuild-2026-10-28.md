# A1 rebuild plan, 28 October 2026

The user tried the first A1 bank and found it too hard, partly because of individual words. Nobody has used A1 yet, so every existing A1 exercise is rewritten under its own ID, and the bank grows to 30 reading, 30 listening, 24 speaking and 24 writing exercises. Authors follow `docs/briefs/author.md`, `content/blueprint.md` §4.0 (above all the "Simplified A1 profile" subsection) and this plan. Reviewers follow `docs/briefs/review.md`.

## Rules every batch shares

- `npm run batch:check -- content/batches/<NNN>-original.json` must end without failures. It enforces the word list in `config/a1-vocabulary.json` for every learner-visible field and the starters, the sentence and field lengths and one picture per exercise. Do not edit the list. If a scene really needs a word that is not on it, choose another scene or a plainer phrase, and record the wish under "Requested words" in the notes.
- Simpler than A2, always. A beginner after a few weeks of lessons must understand every sentence without a dictionary. Prefer the most common word: "de dokter" rather than "de huisarts" unless the place matters; "de winkel" rather than a shop type; "kopen" rather than "bestellen".
- Every exchange is real (blueprint §4.0, "Real premises"). Never a classroom role-play, never a message to someone who stands beside the learner, never a message about something the recipient can already see.
- Speaking picture tasks need no pretext such as a phone call: "Kijk naar het plaatje." plus two direct questions is enough, with at most one short situation sentence.
- Address the learner with **u** in situations, questions and prompts. Characters use **je** or **u** as their relationship requires.
- Names: use only the batch's pool below, plus unnamed roles ("de buurvrouw", "de dokter", "een man"). A name appears in at most two exercises of the batch, and the three exercises of a set use different people. No voice names (Roos, Lieke, Ruben, Daan, Ruth, Jerry, Robert, Arjen, Hanneke, Peter) and none of the older recurring cast (Sem, Karim, Fatima, meneer De Vries, mevrouw Bakker, Julio, Amina, Modibo, Sabrina, Hasan). A named person drawn in two exercises keeps the same three visual traits in both briefs; write them in the notes.
- Pictures: one flat house-style drawing per exercise (`config/illustration.json`). Closed exercises: the picture shows the setting and never answers a question or favours a distractor. Open exercises: the picture supplies the visible facts that the goals ask for. Every brief ends with "No text, letters, numerals, signs, clock faces or logos."
- Listening speakers use roles from `config/voices.json` that fit gender and age; two different roles per conversation; never pair `m-adult` (Robert) with `f-adult` (Lieke).
- Tone: exactly the number of lightly funny exercises given for the batch; mark each item's tone in the notes.
- Sets: in the notes, propose sets of three (different settings, different people, one domain each or mixed) in the order learners should meet them, easiest first.

## Rewriting existing exercises

A rewrite keeps the exercise `id` exactly and is listed in the notes as a replacement (the reviewer records `replaces`). Read the current version in `content/catalogue.json`. Keep a scenario when it is sound and simplify its words, sentences and questions; replace the scenario when it is weak (the slug may then no longer describe it, which is acceptable). Give every rewritten exercise new people from the pool. Reading exercises from batch 069 had no picture; add one.
