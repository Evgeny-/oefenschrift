# Exam blueprints and content gap analysis

Analysis dated 10 September 2026. Sources: the official DUO A2 practice exams, opened item by item in the official player (`oefenexamensduo.optimumassessment.com`) on this date; the three official A2 Schrijven practice booklets and the 2023–2025 CvTE openbare examens for NT2 Programma I and II in `reference-private/`; the KNM eindtermen in Staatscourant 2024-15802; DUO's inburgeren.nl and duo.nl/zakelijk pages. Where a number comes from a third-party preparation site rather than an official source, it is marked as unverified.

Official material was read for structure only. Nothing from it is reproduced in the catalogue.

## 1. Summary

- There are two exam systems, not one. A2 language exams and KNM are DUO/Cito exams. B1 and B2 language exams are the Staatsexamens NT2 (CvTE), Programma I and II. They have different formats, timings, task types and scoring models, and a learner is only ever preparing for one of them per skill.
- All five A2 formats are now verified first-hand: Lezen 25 questions/65 min, Luisteren 25/45, Spreken 16 tasks in four fixed types/35 min (no multiple choice since 1 March 2025), Schrijven 4 tasks/40 min on paper, KNM 40 questions/45 min in the July 2025 photo-and-fact format grouped by eight themes.
- The bank holds 77 exercises: 92 closed questions and 36 open tasks. Measured against the blueprints it is roughly one A2 reading form, half an A2 listening form, under half a KNM form, and less than one form of everything else. More importantly, several parts have the wrong shape: A2 Spreken has none of the four official task types, A2 Schrijven has one of four, KNM is written as reading comprehension rather than recall, and every "B1" writing and speaking task is shorter than the official A2 e-mail task.
- Reading and listening material is too short. A2 reading texts average 60 words (official ≈ 100–250), B1 texts average 130 words (official ≈ 400–700), A2 listening clips run 12–21 seconds (official fragments are conversations of roughly 30–90 seconds, with the question read aloud).
- Question design is narrower than the exams: 8 of 92 prompts use the persona-scenario form that dominates A2; one question asks about purpose; every item has three options (A2 mixes three and four); the answer key is A 41, B 36, C 15, so C is correct 16% of the time.
- What is worth keeping is the editorial pipeline (author/reviewer split, hash gates, evidence quotes, KNM source dates), the evidence-anchored explanations (the official exams give none), the bilingual criteria and model answers, and the app infrastructure. The content itself needs to be regenerated against blueprints, with the existing items reused as drill material after repair.
- The unit that is wrong is the exercise, not the set. A set of three to five exercises is a sensible drill size once each exercise is an exam-sized block (a text with two or three questions at A2, a fragment with two or three questions, a theme block, an onderdeel of four speaking tasks). Section 6 gives set sizes, bank sizes and authoring rules per part.

## 2. Who takes which exam

| Situation | Language exams | Level | Knowledge parts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wet inburgering 2013 (inburgeringsplichtig before 1 January 2022) | DUO Lezen, Luisteren, Schrijven, Spreken | A2 (or a higher NT2 diploma as exemption) | KNM; ONA if plichtig from 1 January 2015; PVT if plichtig from 1 October 2017 |
| Wi2021, B1-route | Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I, four skills | B1 | KNM, MAP, PVT |
| Wi2021, B1-route after afschalen | DUO A2 exam for the skill(s) that were scaled down | A2 per skill | KNM, MAP, PVT |
| Wi2021, onderwijsroute | Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I or II | B1 or B2 | KNM, PVT (no MAP after a taalschakeltraject) |
| Wi2021, Z-route | none | — | 800 hours, eindgesprek at the municipality |

Afschalen from B1 to A2 is allowed per skill after at least 600 hours of lessons at a school with the Blik op Werk keurmerk, recorded in the learner's PIP. The practical consequence for a preparation service: many Wi2021 learners end up mixing B1 and A2 preparation, and DUO reported in May and July 2026 that there are too few examination slots for Spreken A2 and Schrijven A2 and that their results take longer. Demand for the productive A2 skills is high.

KNM applies to both laws. Its eindtermen changed on 1 July 2025; items written against the old "wat kan hij het beste doen" behaviour format are obsolete.

## 3. Blueprints

### 3.1 DUO A2 exams and KNM

**Lezen A2.** 25 multiple-choice questions, 65 minutes, on the computer. Practice exam 1 contains ten texts with two or three questions each. Text lengths are 640–1600 characters, roughly 100–250 words. Every text is preceded by a one-line situation ("Sabrina krijgt een brief van haar werk.") and the instruction "Lees eerst de vraag. Lees daarna de tekst." Questions have three or four options in the same exam. Text types observed: numbered study tips from an opleidingsgids; a letter to all residents of a village; a shop folder with a table of delivery options; a letter from an employer about a staff outing; an e-mail from a colleague with a picture-choice question; a letter from a neighbourhood association with a sports-day programme; an e-mail from a wedding committee; a web folder for a first-aid course; a newspaper piece about a recycling centre; company rules for calling in sick. Question operations: locate a fact (when, where, who, how much, what to bring); apply a rule to a named person ("Gaston woont in de Flamingostraat. Wanneer moet hij zijn auto weghalen op zaterdag 13 juni?"); pick the advice that fits a person; order ("Wat moet hij het eerste doen?"); identify why a letter or e-mail was sent; choose a picture. DUO does not publish the pass mark; preparation sites quote about 18 of 25 (unverified).

**Luisteren A2.** 25 multiple-choice questions, 45 minutes. Practice exam 1 has about eleven fragments, video and audio, with two or three questions each. Each item shows a situation line ("Steven komt Jari tegen op straat. Ze praten met elkaar."), the instruction "Lees eerst de vraag. Kijk daarna naar de video." or "Luister daarna naar de tekst.", a play control, and the question, which is also read aloud. Three or four options; some audio fragments come with a still image. Fragment types: a street conversation between acquaintances (video); a company promotion video; an announcement at a language school; neighbours talking in a community centre; television news items; a voicemail order at a wholesaler; a student talking to a teacher after class; a voicemail from a friend; a teacher explaining a project; two parents discussing the school. The practice player allows replay; the real exam is understood to play once (unverified).

**Spreken A2.** 16 tasks, 35 minutes, on the computer with microphone. Since 1 March 2025 there are no multiple-choice items. Four onderdelen of four tasks each:

1. Video: a person asks you something directly; you answer.
2. One picture: describe and give an opinion. "Sem luncht op school. Vertel wat Sem kan eten. Vertel ook wat u van dit eten vindt. Gebruik het plaatje."
3. Two pictures: choose and explain. "U volgt een opleiding. In welk gebouw hebt u liever les? Vertel ook waarom. Kies een van de plaatjes."
4. Three pictures: narrate a sequence. "Ricardo werkt als kapper. Kijk naar de plaatjes. Vertel wat Ricardo doet. Vertel iets over alle plaatjes."

Instructions are printed and read aloud. Trained raters (Cito trains them) score task fulfilment and content, comprehensibility, vocabulary, grammar and pronunciation. DUO publishes no cesuur.

**Schrijven A2.** 4 tasks, 40 minutes, pen and paper. Across the three official practice booklets (12 tasks): five e-mails with three or four bullet requirements and a printed header, salutation and closing (formal to a teacher or manager, informal to a colleague or fellow student); three wijkkrant pieces ("Schrijf minimaal drie zinnen op" with three guiding questions about a festival, favourite clothes, the weekend); three forms (registration, police report, municipal complaint) with personal details and open fields, sometimes with pictures; one note to a colleague listing three things to do, based on pictures. A typical form is therefore one or two e-mails, one wijkkrant piece and one form. The official model for the short correspondence task scores adequaatheid/begrijpelijkheid 0–3 (0 stops all further scoring), grammaticale correctheid 0–2, spelling 0–2 (1 point when common short words are spelled phonetically and personal details correctly), coherentie 0–1 (en, maar, want, omdat, die, dat) and woordenschat 0–2.

**KNM.** 40 multiple-choice questions, 45 minutes, on the computer. Since 1 July 2025 each item is a photograph plus a short factual question, read aloud, with three options, also read aloud. Questions are grouped in theme blocks announced by a divider ("De volgende vragen gaan over het thema 'wonen'. Klik op 'volgende'."). There are no introduction videos and no "what should he do" behaviour questions. Items are written at A2. The eindtermen (Regeling inburgering 2021, bijlage 2, Staatscourant 2024-15802) define eight themes with about seventy numbered objectives:

1. Werk en inkomen: finding work, contracts, UWV and municipal provisions, rights and duties on benefits, discrimination is illegal, starting a business.
2. Omgangsvormen, waarden en normen: social groups differ, directness, appointments, forms of cohabitation, official holidays and remembrance days, clubs, family customs.
3. Wonen: housing allocation, tenant rights, utilities and their cost, waste separation.
4. Gezondheid en gezondheidszorg: huisarts and praktijkondersteuner, pharmacy, dentist, maternity care, referral, huisartsenpost, 112, compulsory insurance, declaring costs, DigiD, welfare services.
5. Geschiedenis en geografie: history and heritage including the Holocaust, recent history, main places and regions, public transport.
6. Instanties: digital government contact, reporting family changes, official documents, residence and naturalisation information, municipal tasks, payslip and jaaropgave, tax return and toeslagen, police tasks, objection and legal aid, insurance.
7. Staatsinrichting en rechtsstaat: state structure, EU membership, separation of powers, voting rights, equality before the law, freedom of expression and religion, non-discrimination, privacy, bodily integrity, organ donation, women's self-determination.
8. Onderwijs en opvoeding: structure of the school system, freedom of education, compulsory schooling, pre-school and after-school care, compulsory sexuality education, parental responsibility, parental involvement, parenting support, school costs, allowances via DUO.

### 3.2 Staatsexamen NT2 Programma I (B1)

**Lezen I.** 6 texts, 35 multiple-choice questions with three options, 110 minutes; the Van Dale NT2 pocket dictionary is allowed. Texts 1–5 must be read in full and carry five to seven questions each; text 6 is a look-up text (an exam regulation, general college information, employment conditions) answered by scanning, with persona questions ("Stef is zijn Visionpas verloren. Wat moet Stef in totaal betalen?"). The marking model classifies every text by domain (werk, educatie, overig) and text type (persuasief, descriptief, instructief, beschouwend); a form spreads six texts across those. Texts run about 400–700 words. Each text ends with "Wat is het doel van deze tekst?". Other recurring operations: what a person thinks ("Wat vindt Ruud van medische onderzoeken?"), the function of an example, which sentence best summarises the writer's opinion, how one person differs from others according to a named source, applying a rule to a case. Pass mark 23–24 of 35 in 2023–2025 (score 500).

**Luisteren I.** 39 multiple-choice questions with three options, 90 minutes. Five audio texts and one video text; each text has a printed and spoken introduction and consists of several fragments. The candidate gets 25 seconds to read each question, hears the fragment once without pausing, then answers. Text types informatief, beschouwend, persuasief, instructief; 2025 examples: a study-choice conversation with a dean, an interview about a traffic-calmed street, a performance review, an interview with a museum attendant, a conversation with a personal trainer, a video with a teaching assistant. Pass mark 26 of 39 in 2025.

**Schrijven I.** 12 tasks, 100 minutes, dictionary allowed. Three task types, published as such on the official practice portal:

- Zinstaak, tasks 1–8: complete one sentence inside a short e-mail or message. The marking model specifies the structure that earns the grammar point: a main clause, a subordinate clause, inversion, a te + infinitive construction. Adequacy 0–1 and grammar 0–1.
- Deelschrijftaak, tasks 9–10: fill a form with six open questions ("Geef minimaal twee redenen"), or complete an e-mail using all the pictures. Adequacy 0–3, grammar 0–2, spelling, coherence and word use 0–1 each.
- Korte schrijftaak, tasks 11–12: an e-mail with five or six bullet requirements, often built on a table or pictures ("De tabel komt niet bij uw e-mail. De tekst moet dus te begrijpen zijn zonder de tabel."), with a stated goal such as convincing a shop or a fellow student. Same scales as the deelschrijftaak.

Totals: adequaatheid/begrijpelijkheid 20, samenhang 3, woordgebruik 4, grammaticale correctheid 16, spelling 4; maximum 47, pass 25. Rules that matter for feedback: sentences copied from the prompt earn nothing; a text with 0 for adequacy is not scored further; article-gender errors are tolerated but a missing article counts; word order must be correct; a misread stimulus caps adequacy at 2.

**Spreken I.** 16 tasks, about 25 minutes. Deel 1: eight korte taken with 20 seconds of speaking time, often after a spoken cue ("U hoort eerst uw vriend."): describe a picture, ask something, state a preference with a reason, tell someone what to do. Deel 2: eight middellange taken with 15 seconds of preparation and 30 seconds of speaking: narrate three pictures using all of them, convince someone with two reasons, advise with at least two points, instruct a colleague from pictures. Scoring: Inhoud 32, Woordkeus 8, Woordenschat 24, Woord- en zinsvorming 32, Uitspraak 24, Tempo 8; maximum 128, pass 75. A preconditie gate (the answer must be about the situation) precedes scoring. Reading the prompt aloud caps content at 1; performing the wrong speech act (describing when asked to instruct) caps content at 1; the argument's validity is not judged. Small errors (articles, plurals, diminutives) are distinguished from larger ones (verb conjugation, word order, dropped "het"); longer answers of three or more sentences are scored on a more tolerant scale.

### 3.3 Staatsexamen NT2 Programma II (B2)

Lezen II: 6 texts, 36 questions, 100 minutes, pass 21–25 in 2023–2025. Luisteren II: 38 questions, 90 minutes, four audio texts and one video text. Schrijven II: 10 tasks (seven zinstaken, one korte schrijftaak of 50–100 words, two middellange of 100–150 words), 100 minutes, maximum 53 with an added Opbouw aspect, pass 28. Spreken II: 13 tasks (kort 20 s, middellang 30 s, one lange taak of 2 minutes after 2 minutes of preparation), about 25 minutes, maximum 132 with an added Coherentie aspect, pass 73. B2 material is out of scope until B1 is complete.

## 4. What is scored, and where errors count

- Lezen, Luisteren, KNM: one point per correct option, no penalty for wrong answers, no language production. The only "error" is choosing a distractor, so distractor logic (nearby detail, reversed condition, wrong day, wrong person) is the whole difficulty.
- Schrijven: adequacy is judged first and tolerantly (truth of the content does not matter, the intention must be clear and every requested element present). Then grammar (word order, congruence, prepositions, er-constructions, missing articles; gender errors tolerated), spelling, coherence through signal words, and word choice. Copied prompt sentences score nothing. At B1 the sentence tasks target specific structures.
- Spreken: an on-topic precondition, then content (speech act realised, required number of reasons or pictures, clarity, use of the speaking time), word choice ("een pot met dat spul" scores 0), vocabulary, word and sentence formation, pronunciation and tempo. Reading aloud is penalised.

Feedback in the app should mirror these scales rather than a generic "good/could be better" verdict: for writing, adequacy → grammar → spelling → coherence → vocabulary; for speaking, precondition → content → word choice → sentence formation → pronunciation note.

Further measurements across the 92 closed questions: 8 prompts use a persona scenario; 1 asks about purpose; all have three options; the key distribution is A 41, B 36, C 15. The 36 open tasks all have exactly three criteria. Domains cluster on buurt, vrijwilligers and gemeente; the official A2 forms lean on work, training, shops, school and children, health and neighbours, and the B1 forms are balanced across werk, educatie and overig.

The practice sets group three to five of these exercises (8–10 questions, roughly six minutes of reading) with no time reference and no task-type identity. The "Practice test" offers every available closed question of a part in one sitting, which is 26 questions for A2 reading and 9 for B1 listening; neither resembles an exam form.

### 5.1 Worth keeping

- The evidence model: every key is tied to a verbatim quote and an explanation. The official exams give no explanations at all, so this is the product's main advantage in review mode.
- The editorial process: separate author and reviewer, hash-gated integration, distractor rationales, KNM source URL and review date on every item. KNM changed in July 2025 and will change again; the dating discipline is necessary.
- Bilingual criteria, model answers and reviewed sentence starters for open tasks.
- Natural, plausible everyday Dutch without copied official wording.
- The application: stable IDs and URLs, resumable sessions, structured feedback, admin drafts, recording and transcription.

### 5.2 Wrong and worth redoing

- Item shape per part, as in the table: Spreken, Schrijven and KNM need new task types; reading and listening need exam-length stimuli.
- Level labelling: the "B1" open tasks are A2-shaped and should be re-tagged as A2 drills; B1 tasks must be written to the official types.
- Question design: persona scenarios, purpose questions, three-and-four-option mixes, balanced keys, picture answers, and at B1 opinion, inference and look-up items.
- Sets: derive them from blueprints so that a set has an identity (part, task type, domain, length, time) and a mock is a frozen full form.

## 6. Target design

### 6.1 Item taxonomy

Every item carries: exam (`duo-a2`, `nt2-i`, `nt2-ii`, `knm`), part, task type from the official list, domain (A2: werk, opleiding, wonen en buurt, gezondheid, winkels en diensten, instanties, vrije tijd en familie; B1: werk, educatie, overig), text type for B1 (persuasief, descriptief, instructief, beschouwend), operation (detail, time or place, quantity, rule application, purpose, opinion, inference, sequence, picture), stimulus length band, media (text, audio, video-substitute, picture), option count, key letter, KNM eindterm number, and the existing status fields. Tags make coverage measurable and let sets be assembled by rule instead of by hand.

### 6.2 Blueprints as data

Store one blueprint per exam part (`content/blueprints/duo-a2-lezen.json` and so on) declaring block counts, questions per block, options, timing, media and playback rules, scoring model and the domain or text-type spread. The build checks each mock form against its blueprint and reports bank coverage per tag.

### 6.3 Set types and sizes

| Set type | Purpose | Size per part | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Drill (oefenset) | one task type, usually one domain; explanation after each item; hints allowed | A2 Lezen: 3–4 texts, 8–10 Q. A2 Luisteren: 4 fragments, 8–10 Q. KNM: one theme, 10 Q. A2 Schrijven: 1 task. A2 Spreken: one onderdeel, 4 tasks. B1 Lezen: 2 texts, 10–13 Q. B1 Luisteren: 1 text, 5–9 Q. B1 Schrijven: 4 zinstaken, or 1 deelschrijftaak, or 1 korte schrijftaak. B1 Spreken: 4 korte or 3 middellange taken | 10–25 min |
| Half form (deeltoets) | exam rhythm and timing without the full hour; explanations at the end | half of the blueprint at half the time | 20–55 min |
| Mock (proefexamen) | frozen full form, official time, playback and dictionary rules, explanations only after submission | the blueprint exactly | official |

The block, not the count, was the problem in the current sets. A drill of three to four exam-sized blocks takes a quarter of an hour and feels like a session; the same count of 60-word texts takes six minutes.

### 6.5 Authoring rules taken from the official items

Reading and listening
- Open every block with a one-line situation naming a person and a source, then the standard instruction line. Reuse the persona in the questions.
- A2: 100–250 words per text, two or three questions, at least one persona-scenario question per text, one purpose question per two or three texts, a mix of three and four options, at least one table or folder layout and one picture-answer item per form.
- B1: 400–700 words, five to seven questions, one "Wat is het doel van deze tekst?" per text, one opinion or inference item per text, six texts spread over werk/educatie/overig and the four text types, text 6 as a look-up text with scenario questions.
- Balance keys so no letter exceeds 40% and none falls below 25% within a form; place distractors on nearby details, reversed conditions, wrong days or persons.
- Listening scripts as two-voice conversations where the official fragment is a conversation; 30–90 seconds at A2, longer multi-fragment texts with a spoken introduction at B1; record the question audio as well; add a still image where the official item has one; substitute video with audio plus one still and say so.

Speaking
- A2: exactly the four types with the official instruction pattern ("Vertel … Vertel ook … Gebruik het plaatje."), spoken instruction, licensed or generated stills, a recording window of about 30–40 seconds, and a checklist that mirrors the type (both parts of a two-part question, both pictures mentioned, all three pictures covered).
- B1: korte taak with a spoken cue and 20 seconds, middellange taak with 15 seconds of preparation and 30 seconds, the required number of reasons or pictures stated in the prompt; feedback in the official order: precondition, content, word choice, vocabulary, sentence formation with small versus larger errors, pronunciation note, tempo.

Writing
- A2: e-mails with the printed header, salutation and closing; forms with personal details plus open fields; wijkkrant pieces with three guiding questions; picture notes; a printable version for pen-and-paper practice; feedback on the five official scales, with adequacy first and copying of prompt text flagged.
- B1: zinstaken with an explicit grammar target per item, deelschrijftaken (six-question forms, picture completions), korte schrijftaken with five or six elements and a table or pictures; feedback on adequaatheid, grammatica, spelling, samenhang and woordgebruik.

KNM
- Photo plus a question of at most twenty words at A2 level, three options, question audio; each item mapped to one eindterm; theme blocks; a primary government source and review date; a yearly refresh and immediate retirement when a rule changes. Keep the current passage-style items as study cards, not as exam items.

### 6.6 Learning logic

Per exam part, in this order: a "Zo werkt het examen" page built from the blueprint with one worked example per task type; drills by task type from short and explicit to long and inferential; drills by domain; a half form; a frozen mock; a review that groups mistakes by operation tag and points to the matching drill. Progress is reported as coverage of task types and domains, not as exercises completed, and the app continues to avoid pass predictions.

## 7. What to do with the existing 77

- A2 reading (13): keep as drills after extending each text to at least 100 words with a realistic second paragraph, adding a persona or purpose question, adding four-option variants and rebalancing keys.
- A2 listening (7): rewrite as 30–60 second conversations with two or three questions, regenerate audio, add question audio; the existing scripts can survive as the first fragment of a longer text.
- A2 writing (11): keep as e-mail drills with the header scaffold; write forms, wijkkrant pieces and picture notes anew.
- A2 speaking (11): retire from A2. The prompts fit the B1 korte taak (respond to a spoken cue) once a cue is recorded and a 20-second window is applied.
- B1 writing (7) and B1 speaking (7): re-tag as A2 drills; write B1 tasks to the three and two official types.
- B1 reading (10) and listening (3): keep as warm-up drills labelled as short texts; write blueprint-length texts anew.
- KNM (8): convert to study cards; write exam-shaped items from the eindtermen list.

## 8. Sequence

1. Write the blueprint files and the tag schema; add coverage reporting to `npm run content:verify`.
2. A2 Lezen and Luisteren launch banks, because closed items are cheapest to validate and the evidence model already works.
3. KNM launch bank in the July 2025 format, one theme at a time, with source dating.
4. A2 Schrijven and Spreken in the official task types, with rubric-aligned feedback; these are the parts with the largest exam-slot backlog.
5. B1, one skill at a time, starting with Lezen and Schrijven (their marking models are the most explicit).
6. B2 only after B1 has two mocks per skill.

Difficulty remains an authoring target until an NT2 teacher and a learner pilot confirm it; see `feasibility.md` for the validation plan.
