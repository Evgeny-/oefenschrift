# Design direction 02

The first proposal was rejected because it looked obviously AI-generated. The replacement treats the service as a place to work through Dutch exercises. It opens to the reading library. A persistent subject navigator makes the scope visible, while the main area carries the actual material.

Use Source Sans 3 throughout: its open shapes and readable body text suit language learners. The initial palette is white, cool grey and dark blue, with a blue action colour. Dark appearance follows the host. Navigation has a lightly tinted surface; reading passages sit on a plain surface with comfortable line lengths. A single sentence explains each view. Exercise counts come from the original pilot.

The exercise catalogue becomes a focused reading view when a text is opened. Feedback appears beside the learner's answer. The source passage keeps its position as the next question appears. Writing and speaking previews demonstrate a missing requirement in an otherwise understandable response.

Critique before implementation: an early idea used large type with numbered rows and dividing rules. That was too close to another common generated template, and the numbering had no meaning for independently selectable texts. The revised layout uses familiar application navigation and ordinary text titles. Contrast comes from the reading material and selected controls. Alternative controls compare interface typefaces and accent colour without adding product settings.

Guidance consulted on 9 September 2026:

- [Anthropic frontend-design skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/blob/main/skills/frontend-design/SKILL.md): ground visual decisions in the subject, use real content, and critique recurring template choices.
- [NN/g: Aesthetic and Minimalist Design](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/aesthetic-minimalist-design/): retain what people need for their task and remove distracting material.

These sources guide design judgment; they do not establish that a particular appearance will satisfy the user. The next review is visual.
